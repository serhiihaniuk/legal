# Legalizacja logo brief

## Project description

Legalizacja is a Ukrainian-language reference app for people who work with foreigners' residence and employment cases in Poland. It brings together a map of the administrative process, Polish legal acts and their official PDFs, explanations of legal terms, document examples and detailed case guides.

The app helps readers understand how a fact in a case connects to a legal rule, its conditions, supporting evidence, a deadline and the resulting decision or remedy. Polish legal terminology stays in Polish, with clear Ukrainian explanations. Readers can open any topic directly. It is a professional reference, not a law firm, government service or automatic system for deciding eligibility.

## Visual context

The homepage has a white background, generous space, large black sans-serif typography and a full-height particle animation in blue, red and yellow. Interior pages are quiet documents with restrained navigation. The logo should work in both settings and remain recognizable without animation or color.

## Prompt for GPT Images

Create a distinctive logo for an app named "Legalizacja".

Legalizacja is a professional reference app that explains Polish immigration, residence and employment law in Ukrainian. Its users work with legal documents and administrative cases. Its core idea is understanding how facts, rules and evidence connect. The brand should feel precise, clear, thoughtful and approachable.

Design one primary direction: a compact abstract symbol paired with the exact wordmark "Legalizacja" in a carefully spaced contemporary sans-serif. Explore a simple path or two connected strokes that subtly suggest an L and a section sign (§). The symbol must have a clear silhouette and work as a small favicon. Treat this as a graphic design problem; avoid a literal illustration of legal work.

The website uses a white background, large black typography and fine moving particles. Take inspiration from the spaciousness and restraint of the Google Antigravity reference, while creating an original identity. Do not copy its symbol or wordmark. Keep the particles outside the logo itself.

Use black as the primary color. One small blue accent is optional, but the entire logo must also work in one color. Use flat shapes and controlled curves. Avoid gradients, shadows, 3D effects, fine decorative details, scales of justice, gavels, courthouse columns, shields, flags, coats of arms and robot imagery.

Output a clean logo presentation on white with generous spacing. Show the primary horizontal symbol-and-wordmark lockup large, plus a smaller symbol-only version beneath it. No marketing slogan, product mockups, stationery, extra brand names or explanatory text. Spell "Legalizacja" exactly.

## Refinement prompt

Refine the selected logo while keeping its main idea. Simplify the symbol until it remains recognizable at 24 px. Improve the optical spacing between the symbol and "Legalizacja" and between individual letters. Remove details that disappear when reduced. Show a black-on-white horizontal lockup and a separate symbol-only version. Keep the spelling exact.

## Final asset prompt

Using the approved design, output only the logo on a transparent background, with no presentation board, captions or mockups. Preserve the exact shapes and spacing. Produce the horizontal lockup first. Then produce the symbol alone in a separate image, centered with enough clear space for use as an app icon.

Image generation produces raster concepts. Rebuild the approved mark as SVG before using it as the production logo, and check the lettering and small-size silhouette by hand.
