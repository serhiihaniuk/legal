# Foreign employer register: preparation

Prepared on 12.09.2026 while batch 96 addresses A1. Peirce read the existing Polish business-register guide, delegation article, posting-permit guide and recorded gaps. He recommends a separate foreign-employer-register guide because KRS/CEIDG and Polish representation rules cannot describe every foreign register.

The reader's question is whether the supplied record identifies the actual foreign employer and establishes its relevant registered status on the application date. Registration alone does not prove employment, actual foreign work, the posting arrangement, wages or the other conditions of the permit.

Sources checked by Peirce for preparation:

- [Regulation 1629](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1629/text/O/D20251629.pdf), physical pages 2–3, §4 pkt 1 and §8. The record must be current on the application date and establish the employer's legal status and form or nature of activity. These provisions do not impose a universal 30-day freshness rule. Electronic records/scans, requested originals and additional evidence have separate roles. The sworn-translation requirement does not itself impose universal apostille authentication.
- [Work act621](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/621/text/U/D20250621Lj.pdf), physical pages 38–39, arts. 40–41. The register does not replace proof of the actual foreign employment, work or permit conditions.
- [Companies House public-register guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/searching-the-companies-house-register) as one possible jurisdiction-specific example. Registered identity, company type, status, address, SIC activity, filing history and officers differ from actual operations and from an old incorporation certificate.
- [Companies House certified certificates](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/order-certified-copies-and-certificates-from-companies-house), page updated 17.08.2026. Optional certified facts and the limited summary statement must not be confused with tax clearance or proof of actual operations. The service does not certify shareholders, shareholdings or PSC information in that certificate. A paid certificate is not a universal Polish filing requirement.

Potential completed example: compare an old incorporation certificate with current register information, then show the preparer's completed identification note matching the foreign employer's number, legal name, type and status to its employment and service documents. Use a separate fictional company. Do not change the existing Indian permit or German exemption examples. Keep actual English field names and explain them in Ukrainian; a Polish comparison note belongs to the preparer, not Companies House.
