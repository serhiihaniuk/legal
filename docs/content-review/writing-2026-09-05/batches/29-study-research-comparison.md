# Study, research and related residence grounds

Changed on 06.09.2026 following the map contents audit.

## Reader question and organization

The old page combined studies, research, internships and mobility under one article range and eight generic fields. It did not explain how those situations differ or what their documents establish. The existing URL now hosts a comparison article with seven named sections. Separate sections explain studies and other education, university documents, student mobility, research, internships and European volunteering. A final section connects the selected purpose to admission barriers and adjacent questions.

This is the branch's parent comparison, not acceptance of every detailed route. Separate detailed pages should be added where they answer an independent question. The contents audit still covers family distinctions, overlapping decision pages, missing topics and other map rewrites. No empty child pages were created to make the index look complete.

The article links to the existing admission confirmation, university MOS attachment, study-progress and tuition-payment guides through typed document references. Completed fictional examples explain a missing mobility notification record and the difference between a job description and an internship's educational program. The excerpts are explicitly illustrative and contain no client data.

## Source verification

Checked on 06.09.2026:

- [Sejm updated foreigners-act text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1079/text/U/D20251079Lj.pdf), dated 07.04.2026. Read the relevant parts of articles 106, 144, 149b, 151, 151b, 156b, 157a, 157b, 157g and 157h. The article distinguishes permits from notification-based mobility, the roles of institutions, the contract content and bounded duration rules. It also retains the distinction between selecting a purpose and satisfying general admission/refusal rules.
- [Original-act ELI API](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2013/1650) still lists amendments 2026/203 and 2025/1794 alongside the earlier 2025 changes. The updated text includes those amendments. Checked [2025/1794 commencement metadata](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1794/ogl); the new university attachment is described for the electronic filing procedure, without treating old paper guidance as the current filing workflow. The atlas-wide baseline was not changed.
- [UdSC on changing study form](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/zezwolenie-na-pobyt-czasowy--przekwalifikowanie-wniosku) supports the distinction between full-time studies and other education. The page attributes this explanation to UdSC rather than representing its wording as a statutory quotation.
- [UdSC university Q&A](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/qa-dla-uczelni) and [electronic attachment notice](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/informacje-dla-jednostek-prowadzacych-studia) explain who fills and signs the MOS attachment and why a student's uploaded certificate does not replace it. The general MOS Q&A search result confirmed continuation of earlier proceedings; opening that general page failed, so it was not used for additional claims.

This review does not accept detailed financial thresholds, every refusal ground, every mobility condition, all employment exemptions or residence-period calculations. Those require their own explanations and source checks. Independent legal review remains pending.
