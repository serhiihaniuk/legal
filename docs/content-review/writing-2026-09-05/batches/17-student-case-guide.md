# Student case, financial evidence and notice of ended studies

## Question and completed work

The learner needs to determine whether a particular programme supports a student residence application, assemble its evidence, answer a request about tuition and funds, and explain the resulting permit. The previous route combined several educational purposes without following a sufficiently specific case.

Read the complete student route and its replacement files against `legal-explanatory-writing`. The six stable stages now follow a fictional adult applicant from India, already lawfully in Poland, beginning full-time master's studies at a public academic university. The case is expressly outside UKR, a previous Blue Card and student mobility. It distinguishes those situations rather than treating every educational document as the same basis.

- The applicant files on 17 August 2026. Tuition of 6,000 zł was paid in two instalments, but the first submission includes only one receipt. The missing receipt and the university's allocation of both payments explain the evidence gap. Actual nonpayment would require a different response.
- A worked budget separates maintenance, housing, future tuition and return costs. The assumed fifteen-month calculation is 34,500 + 12,000 + 2,500 = 49,000 zł against a 50,000 zł bank balance. The balance follows the two tuition payments, so the already paid 6,000 zł is not deducted again. This is a calculation for stated fictional circumstances, not a universal required student balance.
- The guide distinguishes the electronic university annex from supporting certificates and progress records. It explains the ordinary 340 zł stamp duty, the separate card fee and the need to prove current study for the study-based reduction.
- The fictional evidence request is delivered to the representative on 20 August; its fourteen-day period ends on 3 September. A response on 1 September addresses both payment and funding. Personal appearance, the authority's processing period and the applicant's response deadline remain separate.
- The decision stage covers permit duration, the distinct bases for working, changed or ended studies, notification, appeals and half-counting specified study periods toward long-term resident status.
- All 32 document entries are reused at the relevant stages and resolve to canonical guides with reverse case references. Conditions, deadlines and alternatives remain in the existing folded reference sections. A broken map link was corrected; the coverage test now checks map destinations in the reviewed cases.

The route overview, document definitions, stages and reference material are separate authored modules. They reuse the twenty common temporary-residence definitions, with student-specific overrides for representation and fees.

A new 59th document guide, `temporary-residence-notification`, explains the applicant's art. 113 notice. Its fictional Polish fragment names the ended study purpose, the removal date and the supporting university document. It separates the applicant's notice from the university's art. 149 ust. 3 duty and from changes during an application that has not yet been decided. The guide also states the limited next-permit consequence in art. 100 ust. 2.

The income-evidence guide now has a student-specific explanation and a filled four-row bank-document comparison. Its existing PIT and regular-income material was read for consistency; the earlier batch 08 checks remain its source evidence. The new table is a working comparison, not a reproduction of a bank's form. The notice is a selected fictional fragment, not an official form or a ready-to-submit letter containing identifying details.

## Official checks and date limits

Checks were made on 6 September 2026. Exact passages were read in the official texts or in the official reading PDFs retained from the preceding batches. The new student-specific rules were checked alongside the common procedure already reviewed in batches 09, 14, 15 and 16.

- [Foreigners act, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1079](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1079/ogl), official reading PDF dated 7 April 2026. Read art. 144–149b for programme, institutional approval or exemption, financial conditions, duration, initiation restrictions, refusal, withdrawal, progress data and mobility. Read art. 106 ust. 7 for the university annex and progress information. The current MOS text is read with its commencement date, not treated as effective merely because it appears in brackets in this PDF. Art. 113 and art. 100 ust. 2 support the new notice guide; the latter concerns a next application filed before a year has elapsed after expiry of the previous permit or finality of its withdrawal. It permits refusal rather than imposing it automatically. Art. 212 ust. 1 pkt 3 supports the limited half-counting example. Art. 237 supports the study-based card-fee reduction. Common filing, appearance and processing checks from batch 15 remain applicable.
- [MOS amendment, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1794](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1794/ogl), including art. 12 on earlier proceedings, and [commencement announcement, M.P. 2026 poz. 370](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/MP/2026/370/ogl). The new procedure began on 27 April 2026. The fictional August filing uses that procedure.
- [Application regulation, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 553](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/553/ogl), with the original study-annex reading recorded in batch 09, and [UdSC questions for universities](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/qa-dla-uczelni). The university completes and signs its electronic annex; a generic certificate is not a substitute. The original-form page-range issue recorded in batch 09 remains for the corpus audit.
- [Student financial evidence, Dz.U. 2022 poz. 78](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2022/78/ogl), consolidated text of the regulation including the 2021 amendment. Read §§ 2–4. The return amounts depend on where the person arrived from. The India example uses the other-country amount of 2,500 zł without a return ticket. The regulation lists financial evidence, specifies the location of the institution for the relevant bank proof, limits the dates of the specified bank and employment certificates to no earlier than one month before filing, and gives the NBP conversion rule. It is not cited as imposing a universal fifteen-month budget in every case.
- [Social-assistance criteria, Dz.U. 2024 poz. 1044](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2024/1044/ogl), § 1, effective 1 January 2025: 1,010 zł for a person living alone and 823 zł per person in a family. The student provision requires an amount greater than the applicable criterion after the applicable housing deduction. The example uses 1,100 zł for maintenance and makes the assumed housing cost explicit. The ministry's [criteria page](https://www.gov.pl/web/rodzina/kryteria-przyznawania-swiadczen) corroborates the figures.
- [Housing deduction exception, Dz.U. 2020 poz. 1688](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2020/1688/ogl), § 1, concerns Belarusian citizens. It is not applied to the Indian applicant. The preparatory-course citizenship restriction is identified separately in art. 144 ust. 2 and 18; the guide does not present every language course as eligible.
- [Work act, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 621](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/621/ogl), current official reading text obtained in batch 14, art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 14 and 18. A permit under art. 144 provides a distinct work entitlement; a pending first application is not presented as creating that entitlement.
- [Work-permit exemptions, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1620](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1620/ogl), § 1 pkt 12 and §§ 3–4. Read the three-page publication. The full-time-study exemption includes the institutional conditions; the temporary rule for specified unapproved institutions ended on 30 June 2026. Older UdSC material referring to the previous employment statute is not used as the authority for the current work claim.
- [UKR and procedural changes, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/203/ogl), art. 45. The specified temporary-protection exception covers the listed permits, not art. 144. The prior batch 15 reading of art. 100d in the current [special act](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/337/ogl) supports the processing-period qualification through 4 March 2027. Applicant deadlines remain separate.
- [Stamp duty, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1154](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1154/ogl), current reading text dated 3 August 2026, annex III item 2: the ordinary 340 zł rate. [Card fee, Dz.U. 2022 poz. 1583](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2022/1583/ogl), §§ 2 and 4: the 100 zł standard fee and evidence of a reduction. The guide distinguishes acceptance for future studies from proof of already studying.
- [KPA, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1691](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1691/ogl), common procedure checked in the previous batches: representation and delivery, art. 57 calculation and preservation of deadlines, art. 58 restoration, art. 63 filing, art. 64 § 2 formal defects, evidence, file access and art. 129 appeals. The personal-appearance minimum in art. 106e is seven days; the specified evidence request in art. 106f has a fourteen-day minimum. Those rules are not conflated.
