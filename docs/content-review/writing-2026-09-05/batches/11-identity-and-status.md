Reviewed on 2026-09-06 against `legal-explanatory-writing`. Read all four complete guide files and their rendered pages.

## Questions and completed examples

| Guide | Question answered | Filled example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passport | Which document identifies the person now, and which pages explain earlier travel? | A working record compares an annulled passport, its replacement and an application that still contains the old number. |
| Visa | How do validity, permitted days and entries limit a particular trip? | Fictional C/MULT fields with 30 permitted days and two trips. The calculation includes entry and exit days and explains why the later printed expiry date does not provide extra days. |
| Status documents | Does expiry of the physical card end the underlying permit? | A permanent-residence decision is compared with an expired card and an incomplete record of subsequent events. The conclusion distinguishes the document from the indefinite permit. |
| PESEL UKR | Does an old confirmation establish current status and its continuity? | A July UKR record, a September NUE record and a fictional gmina explanation show why the same PESEL number does not settle the current-status question. |

These are filled working comparisons and selected fictional fields. They do not reproduce secure passport or visa designs, official certificates or client documents. The distinction is visible beside each example.

## Case integration

The CUKR guide now uses one shared PESEL record in its two relevant stages and document register. It describes a registry check, with a separate certificate obtained when useful, instead of presenting the certificate as a universal required upload. Four repeated gmina preparation actions share a definition and link to the PESEL explanation. Five passport rows distinguish the passport's own data from the contents of PESEL. The status-stage introduction explains the September identity-confirmation issue with exact source references.

This is focused integration, not acceptance of the complete CUKR route. In particular, the route's remaining deadlines and other legal statements still require its separate full review.

## Official sources checked

- [Foreigners act, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1079](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1079/ogl) and [Sejm reading text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1079/text/U/D20251079Lj.pdf): read the relevant passport, visa and card provisions, including art. 59, 106a, 106e, 195, 211, 240, 242 and 243. Current ELI metadata reports active status and lists the later amendments Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1794 and Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203. The reading text contains both old and replacement MOS wording; the guide uses the activated wording, not the superseded version.
- [MOS amending act, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1794](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1794/ogl) and [activation notice, M.P. 2026 poz. 370](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/MP/2026/370/ogl): the commencement and earlier-proceeding distinction was read in batches 09 and 10. The present guide confines the all-pages digital-copy instructions to the ordinary electronic temporary-residence application. It does not transfer those requirements to every CUKR application.
- [Forms regulation, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 553](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/553/ogl): active status and effective date 27.04.2026 verified. Read the local official PDF's first three pages and the passport instruction on page 6. Section 6 governs PDF copies and file sizes; annex 1, instruction 16, requires all pages of the travel document. These are different locators.
- [Visa Code, current consolidation shown by EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02009R0810-20240628): active status, current consolidated version 28.06.2024; definitions, no automatic right of entry, extension, annulment and revocation. The guide concerns an existing sticker, not an assumed future digital-visa interface.
- [European Commission Visa Code Handbook I](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/1d79f44d-49ba-4847-951e-129f924b1051_en): page 106 of the PDF explains the fields, inclusion of entry and exit days, and the distinction between validity and permitted duration. The sample's dates and calculation are authored, not copied from a real visa.
- [MSWiA entry and residence guidance](https://www.gov.pl/web/mswia/warunki-wjazdu-i-pobytu-cudzoziemcow-w-polsce), also checked in its [official English version](https://www.gov.pl/web/mswia-en/entry-and-residence-conditions-for-foreign-nationals-in-poland): C/D entries and short visits to other Schengen states. These statements were read together with the statute and Visa Code.
- [Population-register act, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 384](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/384/ogl) and [official PDF](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/384/text.pdf): active metadata; art. 8 pkt 24a and 24b on status codes and the reason for granting or losing UKR. NUE is not treated as a universal finding of illegal stay.
- [Transition act, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/203/ogl) and [official text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/203/text/T/D20260203L.pdf): active status, general effective date 05.03.2026, and exact art. 24–26 on PDF page 38. Final art. 25 requires declaration-based registrants to confirm identity by 31.08.2026 and provides the UKR-to-NUE consequence from 01.09.2026. Art. 26 provides the different 60-day rule and its exception. Search also returned drafts with different numbering; those drafts were not used as law.
- [UdSC identity-confirmation notice of 27.07.2026](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/wazna-informacja-dla-obywateli-ukrainy-posiadajacych-numer-pesel-ze-statusem-ukr): read with the enacted act. The broader news wording was not substituted for the distinction between declaration-based and other identity-document registrations.
- [Official PESEL data service](https://www.gov.pl/web/gov/sprawdz-swoje-dane-w-rejestrze-pesel): current and historical data, full and partial selections, sealed electronic PDF, and the distinction between the file and a printout.
- [UdSC CUKR FAQ](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/cukr-QA): standard status conditions, separate child pathway, registry checks, required attachments and gmina corrections. Its attachment answer does not make a separate PESEL certificate a universal upload.

The MOS visa page and generic MOS FAQ could not be retrieved with the web tool. The relevant statements instead use the sources above. These are scoped checks as of 06.09.2026. They do not establish a complete amendment audit or renew independent legal review. The atlas baseline remains 18.07.2026; no corpus edition was promoted.
