## What the current explanation leaves unresolved

The current text correctly separates illegal entrusting from illegal performance and correctly rejects using work-law articles 85–86 as inspection-competence provisions. Those distinctions should survive. However, the reader is told to establish the competent organ and the elements of an offence without being shown either operation.

The most useful missing explanation is the relationship between an inspection finding, its supporting material, the controlled entity's objections, an alleged offence and a later sanction. A `protokół kontroli` is not itself a `mandat karny`, court judgment or work-permit revocation decision. The current generic label "Акт контролю або запит" conceals these different records and their authors.

The two definitions are not symmetrical. The employer definition includes work inconsistent with document conditions, with express exceptions, and lack of a written contract in the specified scope. The worker definition has a narrower enumerated list. A missed transmission obligation should not automatically become an allegation against the worker or an assertion that the worker had no valid permit.

The existing warning about separate sanctions is useful but needs one actual qualification. The presence of a signed contract is different from timely transmission of its Polish copy. Conversely, failure to find a receipt in the initially presented personnel folder does not establish that transmission never happened. The sent content, recipient, event and receipt must be connected.

## Proposed article structure

Use informative section titles and connected explanation, rather than replacing each old question with a longer task. A suitable five-section scope is:

1. What an inspection establishes. Introduce PIP's employment-legality remit, the distinction between evidence and a finding, and the precise selected inspection records. Do not imply that PIP is the only body capable of checking foreign employment.
2. Whose conduct is assessed. Explain work-law article 2 points 2 and 3 with their different elements and exceptions. Separate a company as the entrusting entity from identifying the natural person whose conduct and fault are assessed in offence proceedings.
3. Which obligation the evidence concerns. Explain the selected contract-copy duty and its separate offence. Briefly contrast illegal entrusting, worker illegal performance and an information/transmission breach, without reproducing every notification from the preceding module.
4. How a finding in a PIP protocol is contested. Explain the written, reasoned objections before signature, the seven-day period from presentation, examination of objections and documented correction. Refusing to sign does not stop lawful follow-up measures. This is not an ordinary KPA appeal against a decision.
5. What follows after the finding. Distinguish inspection measures, mandate or court proceedings and separately grounded permit consequences. Explain the narrow article 85 exception. An allegation or correction is not a universal finding of guilt, immunity or full compliance.

Replace the competency-style outcome and "final quality control" positioning with the actual question of reading inspection material. Remove the exercise; retain only authored explanation and an already completed example. Article 73–77 registry coverage can be a short evidence-bound note, not an unexplained long provision range. Do not retain broad seasonal or protection ranges unless their particular mechanism is actually explained.

### Foreign-employment act

[Current compiled Dz.U. 2025 poz. 621](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/621/text/U/D20250621Lj.pdf), editorial date 17.06.2026, 100 physical pages. ELI API status was fetched directly on 12.09.2026. It lists the act as in force and amendments 1794 effective 27.04.2026, 203 effective 05.03.2026, 473 effective 08.07.2026 and 734 effective 20.06.2026. A failed web-tool API open was replaced by a successful direct official API fetch. The PDF itself was fetched and its indicated pages read, not inferred from search snippets.

- Article 2 points 2–3, physical pages 3–4: the actual separate definitions. Points 2(d–e) retain permitted changes and work on another legal basis. Point 2(f) retains the corporate exception. Point 3 does not simply repeat points 2(d–f).
- Article 17(1)(2), physical page 25: transmit the Polish contract through the contract system or its Polish copy through the foreigner-case system before entrusting work; the harvest-assistance exception has its own seven-day rule. The proposed example uses ordinary direct employment, not that exception.
- Article 17(1)(9), page 26: specified authorities can request the listed compliance records in Polish or translated into Polish. This does not impose an automatic universal upload of every personnel document.
- Article 84(1–2), page 73: illegal entrusting carries 3,000–50,000 zł; the worker's separately defined offence carries a fine not lower than 1,000 zł. Do not invent a maximum from that latter paragraph alone.
- Article 84(6), page 74: the listed failures, including article 17(1)(2), carry 1,000–3,000 zł. This is the relevant conditional comparison for the specimen. It is not automatically article 84(1).
- Article 84(7–10), page 74: separate notification/seasonal offences and ranges. Article 84(10) names failure under article 70(1)(1–2) and false information; it does not name every omission under article 70(2) as though the text did so.
- Article 84(11–14), page 74: separate referral offences and per-foreigner minimum rules. Do not multiply every statutory fine by every worker by analogy with paragraphs 13–14.
- Article 85, pages 74–75: the exception concerns only article 84(1) in its specifically described absence-of-valid-stay-document form. It requires both the article 4(2)/(4) duties, subject to knowledge of forgery, and social-insurance registration where required. It is not blanket immunity from every employment offence, and insurance registration alone is insufficient.
- Article 86, page 75: proceeds of fines and use of the `Kodeks postępowania w sprawach o wykroczenia`. It does not establish a general inspection authority.

The [minister's 28.05.2025 communication, poz. 15](https://www.gov.pl/attachment/fc45e79c-2206-483b-9ff2-f1c7d94a1038) was read on both pages. Its item 1(a) activates copy transmission for wojewoda-issued permits on 01.08.2025. The proposed August 2026 event is after that date. ## Proposed completed example and Polish specimen

This is a new fictional comparison, not an extension of the accepted A/B worker chronology. Proposed facts are not yet product content.

A Polish employer directly employs one adult Indian citizen for ordinary warehouse work. A June 2026 ordinary work permit allows the selected employer, role and conditions for 17.08–31.12.2026. A separately checked Polish national work visa covers that period; there is no independent exemption, agency assignment or seasonal arrangement. The parties signed the matching Polish employment contract on 13 August and the employer transmitted its copy through PGP-UZC to the issuing wojewoda that day. Actual work began on 17 August. Language obligations were fulfilled in this fiction; no new language dispute is introduced.

The PIP protocol presented on 27 August records an alleged failure to transmit the contract because the initial folder supplied to the inspector contained only the signed contract. Before signing the protocol, the employer submits reasoned written objections directly to the inspector on 28 August with the actual sent package and its receipt. On 2 September the inspector verifies the matching recipient, worker, attachment and 13 August submission and corrects that finding. These are all completed dates before 12 September. The seven-day objection rule is comfortably met without a weekend-extension problem.

A `letter` sample can contain these selected Polish fragments, clearly marked as fictional excerpts rather than an official national form:

> USTALENIE PRZEDSTAWIONE 27.08.2026 r.\nW przedstawionej dokumentacji brak potwierdzenia przekazania kopii umowy organowi przed rozpoczęciem pracy 17.08.2026 r.

> ZASTRZEŻENIA DO USTALEŃ PROTOKOŁU, ZŁOŻONE 28.08.2026 r.\nKwestionujemy ustalenie o nieprzekazaniu kopii umowy. Umowę z 13.08.2026 r. przekazano tego samego dnia przez PGP-UZC wojewodzie, który wydał zezwolenie. Przedkładamy zachowaną treść wysłanego zgłoszenia, załączoną umowę oraz powiązane potwierdzenie złożenia. Wnosimy o zmianę tego ustalenia. Pozostałych ustaleń nie obejmuje niniejsze zastrzeżenie.

> UDOKUMENTOWANA ZMIANA USTALENIA, 02.09.2026 r.\nPo sprawdzeniu przedłożonych dokumentów ustalono, że kopia umowy została przekazana 13.08.2026 r., przed rozpoczęciem pracy 17.08.2026 r. Odpowiednią część protokołu zmieniono.

The explanatory result is narrow: this actual record disproves the alleged absence of timely transmission. It does not issue a judgment, confirm every workplace condition or establish that every wage has been paid. If the file had only been a draft, the signed contract would not have proved transmission, and the separate article 84(6) question would remain. That counterfactual belongs in the analysis, not as an invented second inspection outcome.

The first excerpt should remain a factual absence in the presented folder. The surrounding explanation must say why converting that absence into "never transmitted" required further evidence. The corrected finding is not a retrospective upload or amnesty for a genuinely late submission.
