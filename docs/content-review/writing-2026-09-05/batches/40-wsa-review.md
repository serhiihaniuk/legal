# Batch 40: WSA review

## Reader question and changes

What does a complaint to WSA change after an administrative refusal, and how does it differ from an appeal or a complaint about delay?

Rewrote the complete article at /map/wsa in six sections. It explains the scope of judicial review, the choice between skarga and sprzeciw, filing and representation, a completed fictional complaint, judgment effects and subsequent remedies. The example connects an insurance certificate overlooked in the administrative file to annulment and reconsideration. It does not promise a residence permit.

The fictional decision is served on 20 July 2026. The complaint is filed on 12 August, before the ordinary deadline on 19 August. The Polish specimen is expressly a partial fictional complaint. It contains no real client identifiers.

The article distinguishes odrzucenie from oddalenie, procedural representation from representation before WSA, suspension from the effects of a favorable judgment, and cassation from a complaint against a court order. It explains the bounded powers under PPSA art. 145a and 149 without claiming that a court can never decide a substantive question.
