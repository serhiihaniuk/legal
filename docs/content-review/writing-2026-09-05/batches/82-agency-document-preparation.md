# Agency referral confirmation preparation

Preparation and implementation, 12.09.2026. The proposal and source findings below were used for the completed guide recorded at the end. The legal-explanatory-writing skill was read. Batch 79's accepted temporary-work article remains the surrounding explanation, not a newly reviewed whole branch.

## Reader question and proposed document

What does the user employer confirm before an agency submits a foreign-work application, who prepares that confirmation, and how does it differ from the agency-user arrangements?

Proposed ID: `agency-referral-confirmation`. Proposed file: `app/data/document-library/editorial/guides/agency-referral-confirmation.ts`. Proposed title: `Potwierdzenie uzgodnienia skierowania cudzoziemca`. Use `kind: "document"` and category `work`.

This is a proposed catalog name for an identifiable document function. It is not the name of a nationally prescribed form. The author must explain the Polish term in Ukrainian and name `pracodawca użytkownik` as the issuer. The agency obtains and submits the document.

## Current official source findings

### Attachment regulation

[ELI status, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1629](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1629/ogl) reports the regulation in force. The [official three-page PDF](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1629/text/O/D20251629.pdf) was read in full. Physical pages are one-based.

| Locator                  | Finding                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Page 1, § 2 pkt 2        | Ordinary Polish-entity work permit: conditional agency attachment prepared by the user employer, confirming the agreed referral.          |
| Page 2, § 5 pkt 4        | The same document function applies to an agency's seasonal application.                                                                   |
| Page 2, § 6 ust. 1 pkt 1 | Seasonal extension incorporates § 5 pkt 4. The separate § 6 ust. 2 exception concerns the earlier worker contract, not this confirmation. |
| Page 3, § 7 pkt 2        | The corresponding attachment accompanies an agency's declaration.                                                                         |
| Page 3, § 8 ust. 1–3     | Electronic document or paper scan; originals for inspection and additional evidence on request.                                           |
| Page 3, § 8 ust. 8       | Foreign-language evidence requires the specified sworn Polish translation.                                                                |
| Page 3, § 9              | Effective 01.12.2025.                                                                                                                     |

The regulation supplies no national template, exhaustive confirmation fields, fixed confirmation expiry, notarization requirement or separate confirmation fee. Do not transfer another declaration's 30-day rule to this document. These attachment provisions do not establish a universal requirement for every foreign worker or every residence application.

### Agency-user arrangements

[Official temporary-workers act PDF, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 236](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/236/text/O/D20250236.pdf), pages 3–4, was checked for this distinction:

- Article 9(1): written arrangements about work, qualifications, planned period, hours and location.
- Article 9(2): user information about remuneration, applicable pay rules, safety and the specified absence of a prohibited referral situation.
- Article 9(3): further written allocation of specified duties.
- Article 11: the agency informs the worker in writing about the relevant arrangements before concluding the employment contract.
- Article 13: the worker's employment contract identifies the user, period and specified working conditions. The agency pays remuneration.

The confirmation and these arrangements have different legal functions. A short confirmation does not automatically establish every arrangement or prove actual daily working conditions. Conversely, the functions need not always occupy two separate physical documents.

### Official administrative guidance

[Dolnośląski UW ordinary work-permit guidance](https://www.gov.pl/web/dolnoslaski-uw/zezwolenie-na-prace-cudzoziemca-na-rzecz-polskiego-podmiotu-powierzajacego-prace-cudzoziemcowi), agency attachment paragraphs immediately below the main document list, expressly gives an agency-user agreement as an example of the confirmation document. The following demand-for-workers item is local guidance, not an additional nationwide requirement inferred from § 2 pkt 2.

[PUP Częstochowa foreign-employment information](https://czestochowa.praca.gov.pl/dla-pracodawcow-i-przedsiebiorcow/zatrudnianie-cudzoziemcow), document-list item 13, also names the conditional user-issued confirmation. Neither checked passage establishes a mandatory nationwide blank form.

Only these relevant passages were relied on. Other office-page statements were not accepted as a complete restatement of the current law.

## Proposed self-contained guide structure

1. Explain the document's purpose and its conditional place in the four filing routes. A compact route table is clearer than four nearly identical explanations. Distinguish the confirmation from the later work instrument and the worker's contract.
2. Explain obtaining and reading it. The agency asks the actual user to confirm the real agreement. Identify issuer, agency, person, work, location and planned period sufficiently to compare the document with the application. Present these as useful identification and consistency checks, not an exhaustive statutory form specification. The issuer's representative must be identifiable; avoid inventing a universal signature technology or company-seal rule.
3. Explain the relationship to the article 9 arrangements. An existing agreement may perform the confirmation function if its contents and provenance support that use. Do not tell the reader to manufacture another document merely because the agreement has a different title. A unilateral agency request that the user never confirmed is a different record.
4. Show a completed comparison of an erroneous initial confirmation and its corrected version. Explain exactly which mismatch was resolved and what remains outside the document's evidentiary scope.

`howToObtain` should identify the agency's request to the user and the return of the user's document. `formAndValidity` should cover electronic documents/scans, possible original inspection and conditional translation. `doesNotProve` should separate agreed referral, lawful work entitlement, actual performance and compliance with the temporary-work period limit. The guide should not become a second agency-law article.

## Proposed separate fictional specimen

This illustration is separate from batch 79's permanent-resident example. That example should not gain an unnecessary foreign-work attachment.

The proposed illustration concerns preparation of an ordinary agency work-permit application, not a declaration or seasonal case. Agency A and user U agreed on 07.09.2026 that a named Indian candidate would be referred for planned warehouse work from 15.10.2026 to 31.12.2026. The initial confirmation of 08.09.2026 mistakenly said 01.10.2026. The user checked the agreement and issued the corrected confirmation on 10.09.2026. The comparison was completed that day. No filing result or work commencement is asserted.

Selected Polish fragment, expressly fictional and not an official form. Real identifiers and signatures are omitted; A, U and the candidate label are teaching aliases, not instructions to omit identification in an actual document.

> Potwierdzenie uzgodnienia skierowania cudzoziemca
>
> Sporządzono: 10.09.2026
>
> Pracodawca użytkownik U potwierdza uzgodnienie z agencją pracy tymczasowej A z dnia 07.09.2026 dotyczące skierowania kandydata będącego obywatelem Indii, oznaczonego w tym przykładzie jako osoba X.
>
> Uzgodniony okres skierowania: od 15.10.2026 do 31.12.2026. Rodzaj pracy: kompletowanie zamówień w magazynie pracodawcy użytkownika U we Wrocławiu. Uzgodniony wymiar czasu pracy: pełny etat.
>
> W potwierdzeniu z 08.09.2026 omyłkowo wskazano początek okresu jako 01.10.2026. Prawidłową datą wynikającą z uzgodnienia z 07.09.2026 jest 15.10.2026. Niniejsze potwierdzenie koryguje tę omyłkę; nie zmienia uzgodnionego okresu.
>
> Dokument sporządziła osoba uprawniona do działania w imieniu pracodawcy użytkownika U. Dane tej osoby, dane identyfikacyjne podmiotów i cudzoziemca, dokładny adres miejsca pracy oraz podpis pominięto w przykładzie.

The Ukrainian reasoning should show that the agency compared both versions with the original agreement and retained the correction history. The completed result is a consistent confirmation of the planned start date. The document establishes neither an issued permit nor permission to begin work. If the parties actually changed their agreement, this correction narrative would be inaccurate and must not be reused.

No pay amount, permit decision, travel status or authority acceptance should be invented to complete this narrow illustration. Those matters are not needed to explain the corrected document.
