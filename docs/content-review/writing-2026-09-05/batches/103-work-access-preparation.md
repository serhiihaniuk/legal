## Proposed substantive explanation

Use four or five connected sections, adjusted to the author's actual prose:

1. Define `legalny pobyt` as presence under the applicable legal basis and within its limits. Distinguish the right from the document which evidences it. A visa, residence decision, physical card and an application receipt perform different functions.
2. Explain how the work basis is identified: a named status, a specific exemption with its conditions, work within a relevant residence permission, or an issued/registered work instrument plus compatible stay. One document can relate to both questions without making them identical. Keep the word "required" beside permit requirements.
3. Explain compatibility through the exact visa-purpose rule. A valid period answers a time question; the purpose answers a different legal question. An ordinary work permit does not rewrite the visa's purpose or legalize stay.
4. Explain the conditions of the actual job. Employer, worker, work period and the recorded conditions must match, subject to applicable permitted changes or another independent basis. Do not assert that every discrepancy automatically has the same effect or that every work model has identical document fields.
5. Explain the selected completed comparison and why the conclusion is limited to the examined day and basis. Remove the exercise and generic evidence lists which merely repeat these headings.

A short contrast with Polish permanent residence under article 3 ust. 1 pkt 6 prevents the explanation from becoming "every foreigner needs two permits". Keep that contrast concise because the neighboring accepted definitions module already explains it. Do not recite all article 3 statuses or all personal exemptions without checking their imported conditions.

The old target includes article 5a as a main reference. The selected example does not involve temporary protection or Ukraine. Prefer an accurate bounded scope reflecting the actual rewritten explanation rather than retaining article 5a merely to preserve the old broad range. If retained as orientation, say only what specific role the notice has and refer its detailed timing and transitions to the existing dedicated explanations. Do not reintroduce a claim that Ukrainian citizenship alone settles the work basis, or that a notification is a separate permit. A detailed post-5-March Ukrainian example would require fresh 2026/203 transition verification and is unnecessary here.

This is a proposed new fiction for author review, not a real case or already approved product example.

An adult Indian citizen X was lawfully admitted to Poland on 1 September 2026 for a tourist stay. The Polish-issued Schengen visa is valid from 1 to 30 September, permits 30 days and covers the actual single stay. There were no earlier relevant short stays, no cancellation/revocation and no intervening change of stay basis. These are stipulated facts for this bounded analysis, not conclusions drawn from the date fields alone.

Company A has an issued ordinary work permit for X covering 1 September to 31 December 2026. The selected proposed work matches A, the worker and the permit's job/contract conditions. X has no independent exemption or other work basis. The employer proposes a first working day of 10 September. On 8 September, the person checking the file completes the comparison and the employer does not admit X to that work. No work began under the examined arrangement.
