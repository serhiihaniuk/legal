# Written arrangements between agency and user

Completed 12.09.2026 using legal-explanatory-writing. The reader question is what the agency and user agree, what the user supplies and what the agency tells the worker before an employment contract. A general cooperation agreement or referral confirmation did not previously explain these distinctions.

## Content and sources

Added agency-user-arrangements with four substantive sections and a completed 8/10/11 September preparation example. Its selected Polish specimen separates five agreed work conditions, user information, reporting arrangements and non-transferable statutory duties. The chosen third-working-day reporting deadline is explicitly contractual. Planned October–November work remains unperformed; written notice to the worker does not imply a signed employment contract.

[The temporary-workers act, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 236](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/236/text/O/D20250236.pdf), physical pages 3–4 and 8, was checked for articles 9, 9a, 11, 11c, 13 and 25a. Bilateral arrangements differ from user information. Article 9(2a) duties are not freely transferred; article 25a does not import the whole employment package into civil contracts. The final 11c wording preserves the distinction for pay and benefits other than those agreed in the worker's contract.

[The attachments regulation, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1629](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1629/text/O/D20251629.pdf), physical page 1, § 2 pkt 2, establishes the relevant user-issued referral document. [Dolnośląski UW's agency attachment paragraph](https://www.gov.pl/web/dolnoslaski-uw/zezwolenie-na-prace-cudzoziemca-na-rzecz-polskiego-podmiotu-powierzajacego-prace-cudzoziemcowi) gives an agency-user agreement as an example. One physical agreement may serve both functions if its contents establish the required connection. Adjacent office statements about fees or formal deficiencies are not imported into this guide.

Checks are scoped to 12.09.2026. They do not renew the global 18.07.2026 baseline.
