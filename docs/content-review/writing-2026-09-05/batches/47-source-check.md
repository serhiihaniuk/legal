# Batch 47: choosing the applicable legal text

Rewrote /map/source-check as a standalone explanation of official publication, editorial consolidation and selection of the applicable wording. It defines tekst jednolity, tekst ujednolicony, obwieszczenie, publication, commencement and transition without turning the page into a generic checklist.

The completed KPA example starts with a proceeding opened on 20 June 2025 and still pending before 13 July. Its six-row source-check card records the question, dates, publication, amendment, conclusion and scope. The reasoning uses the transition reproduced in the opening notice of the official KPA publication, then checks the amending act. It does not infer the applicable wording from the download date or claim that this one transition decides every later amendment or the residence case itself.

The optional reading guide remains a short orientation to the same subject. The map article adds the completed edition-selection reasoning and a reproducible working record. It links the existing case-assessment document guide instead of creating a duplicate document page. The route and primary chapter remain stable. All five topics in the orientation chapter now use authored articles. Legacy renderer tests move to pending-stay in the status chapter; their content-preservation assertions remain unchanged.

No general claim about all publication deadlines or retroactivity is added.
- [RCL training material](https://rcl.gov.pl/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Teksty_jednolite_26032025.pdf), pages 3 and 7–8: character of consolidation and distinction between an officially promulgated consolidated text and an editorial compilation. This is labelled explanatory material, not legislation.
