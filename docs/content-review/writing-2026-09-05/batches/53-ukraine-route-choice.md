# Batch 53: choosing between UKR, CUKR and an ordinary permit

Rewrote /map/ukraine-routes-2026 as a six-section comparative article. It explains the current residence basis, eligibility for each transition, the legal effect of filing, the start and duration of a CUKR permit, employment and address obligations, and separately legislated dates. Its place in the permit chapter and existing navigation remain unchanged.

A completed fictional case follows a worker with continuous UKR, outdated passport data in the register and a pending work-permit proceeding. A filled five-row case note records what is established and what remains unresolved. The example explains why filing CUKR as a backup can end the existing temporary-residence proceeding. Correcting passport data has been initiated; the example does not claim it has already succeeded.

## Source scope

Checked on 10 September 2026:

- [Specustawa, compiled text dated 22 May 2026](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/337/text/U/D20250337Lj.pdf): articles 42c–42i and 42p–42x. Main and child routes, refusal grounds, electronic filing, register requirements, termination of the applicant's pending temporary-residence proceeding, card duration, collection, revocation, address notification, employment, business and the filing deadline. ELI lists the act as in force and the compiled text includes 2026/635. The latest listed amendment concerns financing.
- [Transition act, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/203/text/O/D20260203.pdf): article 45(1)–(2). The temporary-protection obstacle is removed for the specified work, Blue Card, business and family categories. This does not remove their substantive conditions or open every temporary-residence category. The family sponsor qualification is narrower than a general waiver.
- [Protection act, compiled text dated 18 March 2026](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/223/text/U/D20250223Lj.pdf): articles 109a and 109b, especially 109b(1)(4)(c) and (2). Granting a temporary-residence permit and merely applying for it are different events.
- [CUKR commencement notice, M.P. 2026 poz. 371](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/MP/2026/371/text/O/M20260371.pdf): the technical implementation date is 4 May 2026. This resolves the delayed-commencement notation in the compiled statute. The official MOS commencement notice corroborates availability of electronic applications.
- [UdSC CUKR questions and answers](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/cukr-QA): eligibility, passport data, filing by 4 March 2027 and the pending-case warning. The statutory text controls the precise permit duration and absence consequence. The separate CUKR procedure page failed to load; the FAQ, statutes and commencement notice supplied the necessary evidence.
- [UdSC protection-extension notice dated 6 August 2026](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/przedluzenie-ochrony-czasowej-do-4-marca-2028-r): extension of temporary protection to 4 March 2028. This page is used for that horizon only. Its uncorrected July dates were already recorded in batch 52; the new comparison does not repeat them.

The article distinguishes a card valid for three years from issuance from the permit acquired on collection until that card expires. It gives the statutory absence threshold as at least six months and explains revocation by decision. The separate UKR absence rule must not be substituted for it. The protection horizon does not silently extend the Polish CUKR filing deadline.
