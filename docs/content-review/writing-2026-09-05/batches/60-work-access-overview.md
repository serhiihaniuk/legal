# Batch 60: understanding the source and limits of work access

## Reader question and change

The opening employment article previously named legal categories without showing how to distinguish them in a file. `work-entry` now explains status-based access, a separate exemption paired with lawful stay, and authorization limited to a particular job. It describes what the residence decision, card, visa, permit, contract and work-organization evidence each establish.

The five sections include a completed comparison of four independent fictional applicants for the same ordinary job. A Polish permanent resident, a Karta Polaka holder with compatible stay, an Indian worker whose permit names another employer, and a lawfully staying Ukrainian without temporary protection produce different conclusions. The example identifies both the supporting documents and the missing authorization without treating every applicant as needing the same permit.

The article also explains notification, date-dependent transitions, later changes and continuation while awaiting a subsequent permit. Its closing section describes the other employment topics. Stable route, node and chapter IDs remain unchanged. This is a chapter introduction; detailed instrument rules remain in their own articles.
