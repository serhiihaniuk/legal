# Batch 54: CUKR eligibility, filing and collection

Rewrote /map/cukr-route-2026 as a seven-section article. It answers how a qualifying person moves from protection to CUKR, what the register must establish, what counts as filing and which event creates the permit. The main eligibility rule and the child route are distinct. A short completed child example explains why a child born in 2026 is assessed under the special rule rather than a pre-birth status date.

A filled four-row chronology distinguishes a signed application and UPO, card issuance, information about collection and actual collection. The conclusion records the start of the permit without restarting the card's duration. The example is labelled fictional and does not imitate the MOS interface or an official document.

Corrected the old claim of three new years at collection and the old absence threshold of more than six months. Explained nonpayment, the effect on a pending temporary-residence proceeding, refusal and appeal, the separate address rules during and after proceedings, work and business rights, and revocation by decision. The existing article renderer, route and chapter assignment are retained. The navigation test now expects the rewritten title and still checks chapter entry and Back restoration.
