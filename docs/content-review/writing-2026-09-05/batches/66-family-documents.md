# Batch 66: family documents and the reply about two addresses

## Reader question and completed changes

Which documents belong to the wife, which establish the husband's status and income, and which are added when the authority asks about their different addresses?

The previous guide bundled the husband's decision, card, employer confirmation and housing under family-evidence. Income and insurance also opened broad collections. All six stages now name the actual documents and distinguish preparation, submission, reuse, updates and personal presentation.

The fictional Indian wife is already in Poland on a national visa permitting stay through 30.09.2026. Her Indian husband has Polish pobyt stały. Their marriage was registered in Poland in 2024. MOS filing remains 17.08.2026. The family home remains in Wrocław; the husband works elsewhere in Poland from 01.07, with completion planned for 30.09.2026. The wife does not work while waiting.

The initial packet names her passport and visa, his decision and card, the Polish marriage record, his employment contract and employment/income certificate, May-July payroll and three executed salary payments of 6,200 zł each. This three-month period is an authored example, not a universal evidence period or the EU-resident historical-income test.

The wife has her own issued private policy with applicable terms and insurer-confirmed paid status, current at filing and reply. The initial housing document is the signed family-home lease. No inferred ZUS coverage or automatically duplicated insurance receipt was added.

The summons is served on the representative on 20.08.2026. The fourteen-day period ends on 03.09; the reply is submitted on 31.08. It adds the employer's work-location confirmation issued on 28.08 and executed July/August rent-payment evidence. The letter identifies already-filed lease, status, marriage, income and policy records without resubmitting them. An actual request for originals remains distinct.

The employer confirms work facts, not overnight accommodation or the validity of the marriage. Its September endpoint is planned. A second accommodation contract is not silently made mandatory; further accommodation evidence depends on the specific question or discrepancy.

Three standalone guides include completed fictional Polish specimens:

- work-location-confirmation: obtaining the employer record, work versus residence, facts versus plans.
- private-health-insurance-policy: issuer, policyholder, insured person, OWU, treatment coverage, payment and commencement.
- residential-lease: premises, authority to grant use, period, rent versus other charges, spouses' tenancy rights and payments.

The policy and lease specimen amounts and dates are expressly separate illustrations, not additional family-case facts. Foreign-family paper applications, consent, authentication and translations remain outside this selected MOS packet. Issued art. 108 filing confirmation is reviewed separately from UPO. Remedies and later changes retain conditional roles.
