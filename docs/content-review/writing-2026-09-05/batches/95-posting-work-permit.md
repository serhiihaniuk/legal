Official sources checked for 12.09.2026:

- [Current work act, Dz.U.2025 poz. 621](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/621/text/U/D20250621Lj.pdf), physical pages 38–40, arts. 40–44. The employer must employ the worker abroad and actually receive their work there. The purpose distinguishes an affiliated recipient, export service and another purpose excluding service. Art.40 ust. 4–5 determines the wojewoda. Art.41 uses pkt; art. 42 uses ust. 1–2 for fields and recipient; art. 44 uses pkt for its different exemptions.
- The same act, physical pages 25–28, arts. 17–21: reflected conditions, contract transmission, current posting wage adjustment, delivery of the decision to the worker, qualified changes and continuation. Art.18's listed identity/name changes must not be confused with an actual new recipient or different work. Art.33's additional flexibility concerns Polish-employer permits.
- [ELI base-act metadata](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/621/ogl): act in force from 01.06.2025. The checked compiled text includes2025/1794 and 2026/203, 473, 734; Copernicus checked the current API amendment list.
- [Attachment regulation, Dz.U.2025 poz. 1629](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1629/text/O/D20251629.pdf), §4 and§8, physical pages 2–3: foreign register, passport copies, actual foreign employment/work, purpose-specific evidence and other stated records. These are application evidence, not substitutes for the issued decision. It entered into force01.12.2025.
- [Dz.U.2026 poz. 1072](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/1072/text/O/D20261072.pdf), complete single-page regulation: nationality-specific visa-free work restrictions effective 22.08.2026 and the specified continuation rule. Root checked it to avoid turning lawful visa-free stay into a universal right to work. It does not describe the Indian worker's residence basis in this separate example.
