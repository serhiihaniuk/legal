# Permanent residence and Karta Polaka

## Question and completed work

The reader needs to identify the exact permanent-residence ground, connect its facts to documents and follow filing, evidence requests and document duties after the decision. The same name, pobyt stały, does not imply the same requirements for every applicant.

Reviewed the complete replacement guide and new Karta Polaka document page against `legal-explanatory-writing`. The guide keeps six stable stages and separates the overview, document definitions, stages and reference material. The stages and register reuse 29 document entries. Shared evidence records are selected explicitly; permanent-residence filing references do not inherit temporary-residence art. 106 provisions.

The fictional adult Belarusian applicant has a valid Karta Polaka, lawful national-visa stay through 30 September 2026, Polish work and a one-year lease after moving on 1 August. The signed MOS application and UPO are dated 17 August. The representative receives a fourteen-day request about the settlement intention on 20 August. The reply on 31 August is timely before 3 September. It explains the permanent plan and discloses that the next housing agreement does not yet exist. The example resolves that evidence discrepancy without inventing a favourable decision.

- Distinguishes Karta Polaka, lawful stay, work rights and settlement intention. The card is not citizenship, a border-crossing document or an automatic finding of Polish origin under the separate route.
- Keeps refusal grounds visible, including false information and relevant tax arrears with statutory qualifications. The Polish-origin exception is not copied into the Karta Polaka category.
- Explains different grounds for Polish origin, the spouse of a Polish citizen and children at orientation level. It does not turn these alternatives into a universal attachment list or claim exhaustive treatment of protection categories outside the example.
- Separates a form's five-year travel-history question from a five-year qualifying-stay condition. The EU long-term resident requirements are not copied into the Karta Polaka ground.
- Uses the permanent application and art. 203c–203i rules for signature, UPO, passport, photo, evidence requests and personal actions. Conditional documents and working records remain identified as such.
- Separates the ordinary 640 zł stamp duty and the Karta Polaka exemption from the standard 100 zł card fee. Explains the indefinite permit, ten-year card, subsequent card application and return of Karta Polaka within fourteen days of the decision becoming final.
- Adds the previously missing Karta Polaka guide to the canonical catalogue, typed document IDs, knowledge registry and review inventory. Its filled three-row comparison links each material to an established fact and a conclusion it does not support. This is a fictional working record, not an official card reproduction or a primary Karta Polaka application guide.

## Official checks and date limits

Checks are scoped to 6 September 2026. The atlas baseline remains 18 July 2026. This batch does not renew independent-review attestations or complete the corpus-wide PDF audit.

- [Foreigners act, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1079](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1079/ogl), with the official reading text dated 7 April 2026 and later amendments shown there. Read the permanent-residence grounds in art. 195, initiation barriers and refusals in art. 196–198, withdrawal and expiry in art. 199–200, authority and filing in art. 201–203i, stay during proceedings in art. 206, processing rules in art. 210, and card rules in art. 229–230 and 235–243. The post-MOS text is distinguished from the bracketed old wording.
- [Karta Polaka act, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 76](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/76/ogl), official consolidated text with the stated 13 January 2026 consolidation cutoff. Read art. 3, 7, 17–18 and 20. The return deadline is triggered by finality of the permanent-residence decision, not by receiving the plastic residence card. Loss of validity and the return deadline have distinct statutory wording.
- [MOS amendment, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1794](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1794/ogl), together with the transition readings retained from preceding batches; [commencement notice, M.P. 2026 poz. 370](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/MP/2026/370/ogl); and [UdSC MOS guidance](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/info-mos). This example is a new filing after 27 April 2026, not a conversion of an old paper proceeding.
- [Permanent application regulation, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 488](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/488/ogl), current status and 27 April commencement checked. The original application, fingerprint and signature forms remain available through the existing document guides. Their layout is not replaced with a fictional universal form.
- [Stamp-duty act, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1154](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1154/ogl), annex III item 3, read in the original PDF. The Karta Polaka exemption is specific to the permit duty. [Card-fee regulation, Dz.U. 2022 poz. 1583](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2022/1583/ogl), § 2, establishes the standard 100 zł fee; separate statutory exemptions and reductions are not inferred from the stamp-duty exemption.
- [Employment act, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 621](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/621/ogl), current status and retained official reading text dated 17 June 2026. Art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 6 covers permanent residence. Art. 3 ust. 5 pkt 1 ties Karta Polaka work rights to the specified stay basis or visa-free stay. The guide does not say that any lawful presence automatically permits work.
- [KPA, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1691](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1691/ogl), retained official text: delivery to the representative, exclusion of the delivery day when counting, missed-deadline restoration, evidence assessment and fourteen-day appeal. The example ends on Thursday 3 September 2026.
- [Special-rule changes, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/203/ogl), art. 17 pkt 50, extends the stated art. 100d date to 4 March 2027. The applicant's own response deadline is not suspended.
- [RPO's publication of correspondence with MSWiA about delayed proceedings](https://bip.brpo.gov.pl/pl/content/rpo-cudzoziemcy-postepowania-legalizacyjne-przewleklosc-mswia-odpowiedz) records the distinction between the administration's application of the suspension and possible judicial refusal to apply it. The guide attributes this to that published position and does not promise a court outcome.

The Podlaski Karta Polaka guidance loaded during the initial source check but failed on a later request. The core claims above rely on the official statutory texts, not on that failed repeat request.
