# Family case and evidence of family life

## Question and completed work

The learner needs to identify the relevant family category, distinguish the applicant's status from the status of the family member in Poland, choose the filing procedure and connect each condition to evidence.

Read the complete replacement family guide against `legal-explanatory-writing`. Its six stable stages follow a fictional adult Indian wife joining her Indian husband, who has Polish pobyt stały. Their marriage was registered in Poland in 2024. Her new MOS application is filed on 17 August 2026 during lawful visa stay ending on 30 September. The example does not concern UKR, children or an earlier paper proceeding.

- The family home remains in Wrocław. The husband temporarily works and lives in another Polish city from July through September. An employer confirmation and housing records explain the two addresses. The example preserves both actual addresses and explains the limits of the marriage record.
- The husband maintains himself and his wife. The established monthly income of 6,200 zł exceeds the two-person criterion of 1,646 zł. Contract, remuneration and payment records support regularity separately from the amount. The wife's own valid paid medical policy and the family housing document address different conditions.
- The representative receives a fourteen-day evidence request on 20 August. The period starts on 21 August and ends on 3 September. The response on 31 August includes the explanation and supporting materials. Evidence sufficiency, formal defects, personal appearance and the authority's processing period remain distinct.
- The final stage explains the possible permit's duration, work rights, appeal and later family changes. The completed example resolves the evidence discrepancy; it does not report a fictional authority decision as an actual outcome.
- The six stages reuse 33 document definitions from one register. The paper application and consent are conditional alternatives for the specified cases abroad. They are not extra mandatory MOS forms. Income, insurance, housing, translation and authentication retain their conditional scope. An employer annex is not added merely because the husband works.
- Overview, document definitions, stages and references are separate modules. The route reuses twenty common temporary-residence definitions, with the family fee and representation wording. Six conditions, seven deadlines and five alternatives remain in the existing folded reference sections.

The family-evidence document retains its four-row filled address comparison and aligns it with the case. A second, five-row filled comparison explains maintenance, insurance and housing. Both are fictional working records, not official forms, client documents or universal attachment lists. The original family application and consent guides remain linked; their source-layout limitations from batch 13 remain open.
