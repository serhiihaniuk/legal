# Delivery, calculation and restoration of deadlines

Changed on 06.09.2026 following the map contents audit.

## Reader question and organization

The connecting article explains when a letter takes legal effect, how that event determines a procedural deadline, what proves a timely response, and when a missed deadline can be restored. Six named sections replace the generic fields and repeated checklist. The existing URL and chapter assignment remain stable. Restoration has its own contents entry and a direct link to the existing filled obstacle chronology.

Completed fictional examples distinguish service on a representative from forwarding a copy, collection after awizo from the first notice, email notification from electronic receipt, calendar-day counting, a Saturday deadline, a missing date in a month, timely postal dispatch, and the seven-day restoration request. The restoration example separates timely filing and simultaneous completion of the missed act from the still-required assessment of absence of fault.

## Source verification

Checked on 06.09.2026:

- [Official KPA text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1691/text/T/D20251691L.pdf), dated 04.12.2025, articles 39–44, 46, 57–60, 63 and 129. Read the consolidation and transition notes. [Original-act metadata](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/1960/168) lists 2025/1691 as the latest consolidation and 2025/769 as the latest amendment. The affected individual-service and deadline rules were checked in that text. No new corpus edition was promoted.
- [Electronic-delivery consolidation](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/3/ogl) and [official text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/3/text/T/D20260003L.pdf), articles 41–42, distinguish receipt, arrival and deemed delivery on the day after the uncollected fourteen-day period. The article scopes that rule to the public registered electronic-delivery service. It does not reuse the paper-awizo last-storage-day rule for electronic service or treat ordinary email as a valid filing channel.
- [Original electronic-delivery act metadata](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2020/2320) lists the later amendment [2026/507](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/507/ogl). Read article 10 of its [official text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/507/text/T/D20260507L.pdf): it changes article 152 concerning CEIDG, not the delivery-event rules used here. Its general commencement date is 14.10.2026, with separately listed exceptions. Future provisions were not presented as current law.

The revised explanation corrects the former blanket event-day exclusion by limiting it to deadlines expressed in days. It distinguishes calendar counting from the last-day exception and procedural deadlines from permit validity. Restoration is expressly subject to absence of fault, seven days after the obstacle ends and simultaneous completion of the missed act. The power to stay execution is limited to the situation in KPA article 60 and requires the party's request. The article 59 distinction between ordinary restoration and restoration of appeal/complaint deadlines remains explicit.

Date arithmetic was checked against the calendar and the existing deadline-obstacle specimen. The page's verification date is scoped to this subject; the atlas baseline and independent legal-review status are unchanged. This article does not exhaust public-notice service, foreign service, every special electronic system or judicial restoration procedures.
