# Other purposes and the graduate case

## Question and completed explanation

The old guide did not establish a case. Its profile left the purpose and duration unknown, and its conclusion asked the reader to create a minicase. The replacement follows a fictional graduate of a Polish university through the existing six stages. The concrete question is whether the submitted materials explain a graduate job-search application and how a missing insurance attachment is resolved.

The graduate completed studies on 30 June, received the diploma on 10 July and applied on 13 July 2026. A previous student permit remains valid at filing; its ceased purpose was reported on 3 July. Job applications and a recruiter's reply establish actual search activity. A policy extension existed and was paid on 1 July, but the attachment was omitted from the initial submission. A demand served on 10 August gives 14 days. The insurer confirms coverage on 18 August, and the applicant submits the complete response on 20 August, before 24 August. The example concludes that evidentiary correction. It does not invent a positive decision or treat a missing document as an actual uninsured period.

The route is divided into overview, stages, document definitions and reference material. Neutral temporary-residence records are reused; the stamp-duty entry is overridden to 340 zł and the power-of-attorney wording no longer assumes an employer. The substantive graduate package distinguishes diploma, search activity, insurance, housing and sufficient funds. Research, trainee/volunteer and ICT forms remain conditional references for different categories and are absent from the graduate filing package. Every register reference occurs in at least one stage.

The page retains orientation to completed research, ongoing research, special traineeship, European volunteering, ICT, seasonal work, short exceptional presence, another EU state's long-term resident status and residual circumstances. It does not present these as a universal permit, graduate filing package or full case study for each category. Their deeper explanations remain in the law and document modules under the remaining review scope.

## Document examples

- `job-search-evidence` is a new typed evidence-set guide. A filled three-row record distinguishes a saved vacancy, a sent application and an interview invitation. The table points to underlying correspondence rather than pretending the internal record itself proves everything. The guide explains why there is no invented quota of applications or universal PUP registration requirement for this statutory basis.
- `qualification-evidence` now explains the Polish diploma's role in a graduate application. A fictional Polish working extract separates completion on 30 June from document issuance on 10 July. It is expressly not a reproduction of an official diploma. The existing Blue Card experience specimen remains and is labelled with its own purpose.
- The qualification guide separates the graduate work exemption from the residence basis and from regulated-profession admission. Related document links connect the new evidence page and status documents.

Both examples contain facts, completed reasoning and bounded conclusions. No assignments or course progression were added. The full authored modules and the existing qualification sections were read. The final generated route and both document pages were inspected through the live browser; catalogue and reverse case references were checked.

## Official sources checked on 6 September 2026

The global atlas baseline remains 18.07.2026. These checks cover the new case and document additions, not the whole corpus. Existing Blue Card content retains the checks recorded in batch 10. This record does not create an independent-review attestation.

- [Foreigners act, ELI metadata](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1079/ogl) and [current Sejm text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1079/text/U/D20251079Lj.pdf), dated 07.04.2026 and incorporating Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1794 and Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203. Art. 186 ust. 1 pkt 6 establishes the graduate category; art. 188 ust. 1a requires insurance, assured housing and sufficient maintenance/return funds; ust. 2a requires a Polish higher-education diploma. Art. 190 pkt 3 provides the one-off nine-month period immediately after completion. Art. 190a contains the specific initiation barriers. No universal number of days was invented for "bezpośrednio".
- The same act's art. 186 ust. 1 pkt 7 and 188 ust. 2b distinguish completed research and the host institution's confirmation. Art. 181, 185a, 186 ust. 1 pkt 3 and 187 distinguish short presence, seasonal work, another EU state's long-term resident and other circumstances. The seasonal nine-month cap is linked to the first relevant entry in the calendar year, not the graduate permit rule. The conditional forms retain the source review in batch 13 and their explicit art. 106/106d/106k references.
- [Employment act, current Sejm text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/621/text/U/D20250621Lj.pdf), dated 17.06.2026. Art. 3 ust. 5 pkt 3 covers the specified graduate of a university seated in Poland, subject to the residence condition. It does not restrict that exemption to full-time studies. Art. 3 ust. 3 remains relevant to permissible residence, including the distinct pending-application conditions. The guide does not infer work permission from a diploma alone.
- [UdSC graduate guidance](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/zezwolenie-na-pobyt-czasowy--absolwent) and [Podlaski graduate page](https://www.gov.pl/web/uw-podlaski/absolwent-poszukujacy-pracy) were compared with the statute during preparation. Their current graduate conditions are useful, but older full-time-only employment wording, labour-market-test wording and paper/photo instructions were not copied. Statutory text and the current MOS instructions control the changed explanation.
- [UdSC MOS information](https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/info-mos) confirms commencement on 27.04.2026, personal signing, attachments, retained filing records and the distinct paper exceptions. The new graduate case uses MOS; ICT remains a paper application by the receiving entity. Ordinary filing, personal actions and the certificate reuse the verified statutory records from earlier case batches.
- [Stamp-duty statute](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1154/text/U/D20251154Lj.pdf), current text dated 03.08.2026, annex III position 2 supports 340 zł for the ordinary graduate permit. Other special categories have different rates or exemptions. [Document-fee regulation](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2022/1583/ogl) supports the separate standard 100 zł card fee; an old diploma does not itself establish current-study fee relief.
- [KPA official text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1691/text/T/D20251691L.pdf), art. 57, 58, 75, 77, 80 and 129, supports counting, restoration, evidence and appeal. The evidence table is a practical inference using permitted evidence, not a prescribed official form.
- [RPO/MSWiA correspondence](https://bip.brpo.gov.pl/pl/content/rpo-cudzoziemcy-postepowania-legalizacyjne-przewleklosc-mswia-odpowiedz), updated 26.08.2026, distinguishes administrative application of art. 100d from the identified court-practice issue. The new guide does not equate statutory suspension with the absence of every judicial remedy. The corpus-wide review of this issue remains pending.
