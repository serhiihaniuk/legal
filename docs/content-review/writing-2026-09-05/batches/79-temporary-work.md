## Corrections established from the current file

- The period-limit reference in `guide.procedure` uses `workLaw.article("20")`. That opens the foreign-work act, not the temporary-workers act. The 18/36-month rule belongs to the latter's article 20. Use an explicit typed external reference to the correct act and locator.
- Separate an employee hired under an employment contract from a nonemployee directed under a civil contract. The temporary-workers act applies specified rules to both; it does not make every civil contractor a `pracownik tymczasowy`.
- Explain the permitted temporary task categories. A fixed-term contract or the agency's registration alone does not establish that the task qualifies.
- Explain agency registration separately from the foreigner's right to work. The special two-year service condition concerns agency services for foreigners who require a work permit or declaration. It is not a universal waiting period for all agency work or every foreigner.
- Qualify the current blanket claim that every foreigner must have a work document naming the user employer. A person with free access to the labour market may not need that instrument. Where a permit or declaration is required, match the actual user to the relevant instrument. A change in the same user's name or address differs from replacing it with another legal entity.
- Name the period records and their issuers. A new agency does not restart the user's limit for the same person. The existing article never explains how earlier periods are obtained or combined.
- Distinguish the agency-user written arrangements from the user-issued confirmation attached to a foreign-work application. They have different statutory roles. Neither alone proves actual daily organization.

## Primary sources checked

Page numbers below are one-based physical PDF pages. Official text was read directly, rather than inferred from search snippets.

### Temporary-workers act

[ELI status, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 236](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/236/ogl) reports the consolidated publication as in force. [Official PDF](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/236/text/O/D20250236.pdf):

- Page 2, articles 1, 2, 4 and 7: agency, employee, user employer, permitted temporary task categories and employment/civil-contract distinction. Tasks are seasonal, periodic or ad hoc; tasks the user's own staff cannot complete on time; or an absent employee's duties. A business cannot be user employer for its own employee.
- Page 3, articles 8 and 9: prohibited tasks; written agency-user arrangements for work, qualifications, period, hours and location; user information about remuneration and safety; allocation of specified duties. Do not describe the dismissal restriction merely as any earlier dismissal: its three-month, same-kind-work, nonemployee-reason and territorial conditions matter.
- Page 4, articles 11a and 11b: agency establishes prior combined periods; the person presents employment certificates or other employment-period evidence and a written declaration or the specified certificate for civil-contract periods, covering the preceding 36 months. Article 13 requires the agency employment contract to name the user and period, with the specified working conditions and agency-paid remuneration.
- Page 5, articles 14 and 14a: user organizes work and keeps working-time records; it cannot direct the employee onward to another entity. Its separate per-person period register covers employment and civil contracts and is retained during its operation and the following 36 months. Articles 15 and 16 cover equal treatment and the employee's compensation claim against the agency, with agency recourse against the user. Do not imply that equal-treatment protection belongs only to foreigners.
- Page 7, article 18a(1a): employment certificate identifies the users and periods. Article 20(1–4): general 18-month total within 36 consecutive months, with both agency and user limits and aggregation of employment/civil periods. Changing agencies does not reset the same user's aggregate. Article 20(5–8) separately permits continuous absent-worker replacement up to 36 months, followed by the specified 36-month interval before another referral to the same user. Article 20(9) contains a childbirth-extension exception; the proposed example excludes it.
- Page 8, article 25a, not article 26: specified provisions extend to civil-contract work; the agency issues its own certificate of periods for that user. Article 25a(3) sets issue/delivery timing. This is distinct from an employment certificate.

The PDF contains two versions of article 21(4) with the March 2025 parental-leave amendment annotation. That provision is outside this proposed rewrite. Do not mistake an annotated old alternative for a current rule.

### Agency registration and transition

[ELI status, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 620](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/620/ogl) and [current compiled PDF dated 17.06.2026](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/620/text/U/D20250620Lj.pdf):

- Pages 260–262, article 305(1–4): regulated agency services and registration; the special service scope for foreigners required to have a permit/declaration; two years from entry and provision of agency services; changed register entry. The requirement is conditional on that service scope.
- Pages 264–269, articles 308 and 313: public register and the relevant service date; change-of-entry procedure. A company registration extract from KRS/CEIDG does not establish this KRAZ scope.
- Pages 376–377, article 445(2) and (6): qualifying old entries/certificates remain valid; registered entities already providing the specified foreigner service before the new act took effect are exempt from the two-year condition and must apply for the entry change within three months. Do not tell every pre-existing agency to wait two more years. The general commencement is 01.06.2025; cite the transition if retaining historical explanation.

[PIP agency-service explanation](https://www.pip.gov.pl/dla-pracodawcow/pytania-i-odpowiedzi/swiadczenie-jakich-uslug-wymaga-uzyskania-wpisu-do-rejestru-agencji-zatrudnienia), opening substantive paragraphs, confirms the distinction between recruitment and temporary-work services under the 2025 act. It supplements the statute and does not replace the precise transition.

### Conditional foreign-worker layer and attachments

[Current foreign-work act PDF, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 621, compiled 17.06.2026](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/621/text/U/D20250621Lj.pdf):

- Page 6, article 3(1)(6): permanent-residence permit gives free labour-market access. This supports the proposed example's separation of agency rules from a permit requirement.
- Page 15, article 6(1)(1): contractual agency route where the foreigner is directed to another entity in the work-permit regime. Page 22, article 13(1)(7): refusal where the purported employer is not a lawful temporary agency and the work would be for a third party.
- Page 35, article 32(2): user employer named in the relevant work permit. Pages 49–50, article 62(1)(1)(f) and (4): agency number and user details in a temporary-work declaration. Page 26, article 17(1)(10), and page 55, article 68(1)(8): user may request a copy of the relevant instrument from the agency.
- Page 27, article 18(1)(1)(c), and page 56, article 69 point 1: changes to the same user's identifying data are not automatically a replacement of the user. Article 69 does not supply a blanket exception for switching to another user. Do not import the former pre-2025 declaration rule without a verified applicable transition.

[Attachment regulation, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1629](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1629/ogl), [official three-page PDF](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1629/text/O/D20251629.pdf), in force from 01.12.2025:

- Page 1, section 2(2): user-issued document confirming agreement on agency referral, attached to an ordinary work-permit application when the employer is an agency.
- Page 2, section 5(4) and section 6(1)(1): corresponding seasonal application/extension attachment.
- Page 3, section 7(2): corresponding declaration attachment.

This attachment is not the agency's work permit, the worker's contract or the complete set of article 9 arrangements. Do not claim that every record used to assess actual organization is a universally mandatory application attachment.

## Suggested article structure

1. Who hires and who directs: a compact roles table, permitted temporary tasks, and the difference from a company supplying its own organized service.
2. What a KRAZ entry establishes: service type, conditional foreigner scope and the old-agency transition. Keep KRS identity separate.
3. What the agency and user agree and do: written arrangements, worker contract, pay, safety, working-time record and equal treatment. Explain the actual task records without duplicating the outsourcing article.
4. Why changing agency does not erase prior periods: combined records, 18/36 rule, bounded absent-worker replacement exception and completed comparison below.
5. When a foreigner's additional instrument is required: distinct stay/work questions, actual named user, conditional user confirmation attachment and comparison to the existing documents. Do not reproduce the whole permit/declaration procedure.

## Proposed completed fictional example

Author may refine these facts before freezing the article. They are a proposal, not existing user/client records.

A foreign adult holds a Polish permanent-residence permit throughout the example. User employer U needs warehouse tasks that its own employees cannot complete on time, within article 2(3)(b), not replacement of an absent employee. No prohibited task or childbirth-extension exception applies. U directs the individual tasks. Agency A employed the person for U from 01.01.2025 to 31.12.2025; agency B employed the same person for U from 01.01.2026 to 30.06.2026. There was no earlier temporary work for U in the relevant history. No work for U occurred in July or August 2026.

Agency C considers referral to U from 01.09.2026. Before that date it obtains A's and B's employment-period records and compares them with U's per-person register. A filled table shows 12 months plus 6 months for the same user, 18 in total. Both periods remain inside the relevant 36-month period. A new agency and a two-month gap do not erase them. C and U withdraw the proposed September referral in August before work starts; by 12.09.2026 it has not begun. This is a completed organizational decision, not a fictional authority refusal or a claim that the person can never work for U again.

The permanent-residence fact intentionally removes a separate work-permit application from this specimen. It demonstrates that free labour-market access does not remove the temporary-work limit. Explain permit-required cases in the separate section. Do not invent a C permit or a new declaration merely to fill a document list. If the author chooses a civil-contract period instead, use the article 25a certificate/written declaration and explicitly revise the record names and aggregation explanation together.
