The overview should explain what a general principle changes in a concrete procedural action and how the five topics fit together. It currently repeats short lists of principles and a generic significance test. Do not turn every breach into an automatic appeal outcome or present the principles as substitute eligibility rules. Cover the actual scope of arts. 6–16 without duplicating four full child articles.

Legality must connect the authority's competence, applicable material condition, relevant fact and evidentiary action. Its present claim about the applicant's burden needs grounding in the particular rule, rather than suggesting that art. 7 itself contains that exception. Avoid presenting income or insurance as universal temporary-residence conditions. Distinguish insufficient fact-finding from the qualified grounds for invalidity; include a completed, limited example.

Trust should explain proportionality, impartiality, equal treatment, consistent practice and information duties through concrete records. Separate statutory art. 8 § 2 from practical examples of justified departure. Verify any claim about erroneous instructions and consequences against the actual remedy provisions. Information duties do not authorize unlawful eligibility promises.

Participation needs the ordinary right before exceptions, precise art. 79a scope, access and evidence links, and a completed example of a response changing the record. The existing phrasing focuses on adverse decisions although art. 10 is broader. Preserve art. 10 §§ 2–3, silent-settlement exclusions and relevant foreigners-law exceptions; do not infer that any missed notice automatically reopens or cancels a decision. Read the full applicable reopening requirements before describing that consequence.

Two-instance review must distinguish a second administrative examination of the case from judicial legality review, ostateczność from prawomocność, and reconsideration from an appeal to a higher authority. Verify art. 127 § 1a and other applicable exceptions, waiver, judicial access without reconsideration, and exact authority competence. Reuse the already reviewed appeal/reopening articles for procedural detail instead of duplicating them.

Suggested sequence: legality and trust, then participation and two-instance, then the overview after its child explanations are coherent. Each article still needs current official-source verification, a complete authored explanation and specimen where useful, independent review, and responsive/runtime checks. The broader document, law-module, provision and original-source-fidelity work remains open.

Independent read-only source preparation by Ohm confirmed concrete corrections in the first pair. Art. 7 does not state a general applicant-burden exception; arts. 77 § 1 and 80 explain the authority's evidentiary duties, while a party-specific obligation needs its actual special provision. An incomplete record does not automatically meet art. 156 § 1 pkt 2. Any discussion of invalidity also needs its limits or a reference to the accepted detailed article.

Art. 8 § 2 requires the same factual and legal state. Changed law or materially different facts concern that comparability requirement, rather than an enumerated statutory list of justified departures. Art. 9 information duties do not create automatic eligibility or restoration. Art. 112 concerns a party following erroneous instructions in a decision on its listed remedies, not every office statement. Art. 111 provides the specified completion/correction mechanism and its own deadline consequences; an arbitrary clarification request does not restart every deadline. If restoration is discussed, preserve all requirements of art. 58. Art. 126 governs the relevant extension to postanowienia.
