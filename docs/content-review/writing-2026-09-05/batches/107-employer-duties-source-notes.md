## Current work act

Read the relevant passages of the [current compiled act, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 621](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/621/text/U/D20250621Lj.pdf). Physical page numbers below are one-based. Its header is 17 June 2026; future-marked wording must therefore be evaluated against actual commencement dates rather than the compilation header alone.

The [ELI metadata](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/621) reported `status: obowiązujący`. Its amending-act relationships list 2025/1794 from 27 April 2026, 2026/203 from 5 March 2026, 2026/473 from 8 July 2026 and 2026/734 from 20 June 2026. No later amending act was listed in that response. This is a scoped metadata observation, not a guarantee against every future change.

- Pages 11–12, article 4(1–7) and article 5(1–4): identity data, presentation and copying of stay documents, the limited article 4(5) exception for article 3(1)(1–5), two years from the end of the termination year with longer-retention override, data protection, written contract before admission and its corporate exception. The understandable written version under article 5(2) requires a Polish contract and a person who does not use Polish. It is distinct from the sworn Polish translation of a foreign-language contract under article 5(3). Article 5(4) separately concerns written, understandable union-right information for the named relationships. The article 4 exception does not include the following permanent-resident category.
- Pages 12–14, article 5a(1–6): initial seven days from actual start; electronic channel; competent PUP determined by the employer; statutory system-failure fallback. Article 5a(5)(1) expressly names a contract-type change with a Ukrainian citizen. The other points separately concern position/type of work, reduced time or hours specified in the notification, and reduced monthly/hourly rate specified there. The module retains both the nationality qualifier and the previous-notification comparison point.
- Pages 25–27, article 17(1–5): conditions in the contract, Polish contract/copy transmission, harvest-assistance exception, current wage adjustment, proportionate increase, information and decisions for the worker, documents on an authority's request, correction of noncompliance and arrears. Article 17(2) is the exact list of board members of the named entities, persons conducting affairs of a limited partnership or partnership limited by shares, and prokurenci. It excludes specified points, including contract/transmission duties; it is not paraphrased as every corporate function in article 6(1)(2).
- Pages 27–29, articles 18–20 and 22(1)(6)/(3): listed organizational/name changes and their seven-day notice; non-start within two months of permit commencement, interruption exceeding two months, end more than two months before expiry; separate seven-day submission period. The non-start/interruption exception requires reasons and an intended-use declaration and excludes only the named revocation ground. It does not cover early end or make every permit irrevocable. A sending receipt is not the revocation decision.
- Page 35, article 33(1): different work/position for the Polish-employer permit model, at most thirty days in the calendar year, remaining permit conditions and advance notice. It is not the seven-day after-event mechanism.
- Pages 46–48, articles 57 and 59(1–4): seasonal permits are excluded from article 19. The accommodation rule requires the specified seasonal entry connection and housing provided by the employer. It requires a separate written lease/use agreement, understandable translation before signature, limits on rent and prohibition of wage deduction. It is not a universal employment attachment.
- Pages 54–57, articles 68 and 70: registered-declaration contract, transmission, current pay and proportionate increase, worker's registered document, scope of documents on request, prompt correction and arrears. Start notification is seven days from actual start; non-start is fourteen days from the start recorded in the register. Final non-start and early end are alternatives in article 70(2), without an express numerical seven/fourteen-day deadline. The notification causes invalidation by law under article 70(5). Article 70(4)'s failure rule expressly concerns timely notifications under paragraph 1.
- Page 74, article 84(6–10): distinct offences concerning the specified language, transmission, notification, housing and false-information duties. The module does not assign one generic fine to any missing record, calculate penalties or claim that late correction erases a prior breach.
- Page 97, article 103(3): the new act applies to declarations registered under the previous act. This is distinct from the handling of a proceeding still pending under article 102.
- Page 99, article 109(1–4): staged technical transition and ministerial commencement notices. A statutory reference to an electronic system is not evidence that each named component is already implemented.

## Contract-system amendment and launch

Read [Dz.U. 2026 poz. 734, text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/734/text/T/D20260734L.pdf), especially page 10, articles 5, 6 and 8. The [ELI act page](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/734/ogl) confirms publication on 5 June and entry into force on 20 June 2026. Article 5 replaces the two transmission provisions. ## Ukraine transition

Read [Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/203/text/O/D20260203.pdf), page 39, articles 40–41, and page 42, article 54. Article 40 retains the named earlier rule for qualifying notifications submitted before commencement. Article 41 applies article 3(1)(12) and article 5a appropriately to lawfully staying Ukrainian citizens without temporary protection for three years from commencement on 5 March 2026. The explanation does not equate nationality, a PESEL number and current protection, or require blanket repeat notices merely because the law changed.
