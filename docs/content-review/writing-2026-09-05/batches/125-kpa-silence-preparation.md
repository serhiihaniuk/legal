## An actual current enabling provision

A suitable concise example exists outside residence law: registration of the identification mark of a measuring-bottle manufacturer under article 26(7) of the `ustawa o towarach paczkowanych`. It is an example of the gate in 122a, not a lesson on manufacturing requirements or a document packet for a residence case.

Verified sources:

- Base metadata <https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2009/740>: in force, consolidated status, `changeDate: 2026-03-11T12:40:10`. Listed relevant amendments are 2025/1826 effective 3 January 2026 and 2025/1168 effective 26 February 2026. The returned list is not ordered by effective date.
- Current compiled text <https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2022/2255/text/U/D20222255Lj.pdf>, printed 11 March 2026, 32 pages. Read physical pages 11–13, articles 22–29. Articles 24–26 supply the mark, registration and exact silent mechanism; article 28 confirms there are other production duties that this result alone does not satisfy.
- Enabling amendment <https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1168/text/O/D20251168.pdf>. All three pages read. Article 3 on page 3 adds 26(7); article 9 retains old rules for the specified registration proceedings already pending; article 11 brings this amendment into force six months after publication, on 26 February 2026. The separate January exception concerns article 5, not this registration mechanism. Metadata independently confirms the date.
- Later-listed amendment <https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1826/text/O/D20251826.pdf>. Article 91 on physical page 17 changes the definition in article 2 pkt 2, not the registration mechanism in 26. Base metadata and current compiled text were both checked. This is a bounded relevant-amendment screen, not a review of the whole product-safety statute.

Article 26(7) supplies fourteen days from receipt of the notification and names absence of the notification from paragraph 3 or absence of the decision referred to in KPA 122a § 2 pkt 1. Preserve the actual `albo` wording when discussing the statutory alternatives. For an educational example, stipulate a complete unambiguous notification and absence of both events. This avoids inventing a general interpretation of a mixed case where a defect notification or decision was actually made.

The register is the mark register maintained by the President of GUM. It is not KRS, CEIDG or a trademark registration at the Patent Office. The underlying notification data refer to KRS, but that does not make the resulting entry a KRS document.

No live GUM register, filing form or issued certificate was inspected. Do not simulate their official appearance, claim current portal availability or use real entity/mark identifiers. The statute is sufficient for a short mechanism example with stipulated complete filing.

### Option A: short real enabling rule, preferred if kept concise

Selected fictional facts:

1. The sole applicant is a measuring-bottle manufacturer. A complete, unambiguous notification satisfying the selected statutory requirements reaches the competent President of GUM on 1 September 2026. No old pending proceeding, defect, clarification, suspension, cassatory return or competing-party issue is present.
2. During the fourteen-day period the organ makes neither the paragraph 3 notification nor a terminating act within the meaning of KPA 122b. This is an established fictional file fact, not inferred merely from the applicant's empty inbox.
3. The first counted day is 2 September, the fourteenth 15 September, Tuesday. The selected silent settlement occurs on 16 September under 122c § 1.
4. The file contains the organ's 122e annotation. On 17 September the applicant requests a 122f certificate. On 22 September the organ issues the certificate by postanowienie stating 16 September as the silent-settlement date. Stipulate proper service to the sole party without adding another delivery-calendar example.
5. Completed result: the particular mark-entry demand has been silently settled. The later certificate confirms that result; it did not create the result on 22 September. No claim is made that all production requirements or any residence condition are fulfilled.
