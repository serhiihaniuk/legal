## Official source preparation, 12.09.2026

ukr_filing_review checked the following sources independently of the article author:

- [Work act compiled text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/621/text/U/D20250621Lj.pdf), arts. 40–44, physical PDF pages 38–40; arts. 17, 102–103 for employer and transition context. The three purposes have different competence and evidence. Art. 44's service exemption requires lawful stay and employment in the sending EEA state and usual work there. The two short exemptions do not produce a general exemption for short services.
- [Posting act, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1682](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1682/text/O/D20251682.pdf), arts. 3–4d, 14–15, 24–26. This replaces the expired 2024/73 consolidated announcement. Employment protection, PIP notice, inspection records and nonmember-state employers are separate questions.
- [Attachments, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1629](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1629/text/O/D20251629.pdf), sections 4 and 8. Related-entity evidence and an export-service contract belong to their respective branches.
- [Foreigners act compiled text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1079/text/U/D20251079Lj.pdf), arts. 140–141. Residence remains a separate permission with its own conditions.
- [MSWiA entry and stay guidance](https://www.gov.pl/web/mswia/warunki-wjazdu-i-pobytu-cudzoziemcow-w-polsce), other-Schengen residence documents and qualifying short stays.
- [Regulation 883/2004](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02004R0883-20190731), art. 12(1); [Regulation 987/2009](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02009R0987-20180101), art. 19(2); [Regulation 1231/2010](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32010R1231), art. 1 and recital 10. Applicable social-insurance legislation and its attestation do not confer immigration or employment permission.
