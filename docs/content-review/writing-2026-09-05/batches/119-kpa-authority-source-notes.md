## Completed ordinary exclusion example

This is a separate fictional KPA matter begun in August 2026. The authority's subject and territorial competence is established and undisputed. A is an ordinary employee, not the authority's head, a higher-authority official or a collegial member. The applicant is A's brother. The fact of kinship is stipulated, no urgent act is required, and eligible employee B is available.

The authority assigns the matter to A on 20 August. On 21 August, A discloses the sibling relationship and the direct superior receives that information. The statutory exclusion does not wait until 24 August to arise. On 24 August the direct superior appoints B under 26(1), and A performs no further ordinary acts. On 25 August B performs the next ordinary activity by comparing documents in the file. The private index is updated. No merits decision is invented.

The completed result is replacement within the same competent authority. There is no transfer to another authority, new application, automatic whole-authority exclusion, assumed procedural fee or mandatory civil-status certificate. The working table summarizes the selected records; it neither proves kinship by itself nor substitutes for the superior's assignment. Its dates are not a statutory four-day replacement period or a weekend deadline computation.
