## Concrete explanatory gaps

The opening needs an answer rather than the competency phrase "Розрізняє...". A change may leave the document usable subject to a defined exception, require a notice, require another authorization, or end the old authorization while another basis takes its place. These outcomes are not synonyms.

The current statement that articles 18 and 33 describe cases where a notice is sufficient merges different mechanisms. Article 18 exempts specified changes from amendment/new issuance but retains notification. Article 33(1) permits a limited different role only with prior notification and other conditions unchanged. Article 33(2) lists separate exceptions. A name-only job change is not a change of duties. A worker moving to another group company is not automatically a transfer of an undertaking.

The current PIP sentence has the right 8 July 2026 date but should identify the particular statutory decision and distinguish it from any inspection letter. Its neighboring warning about changed workplace should not invent a universal workplace field for every permit: ordinary article 32, declaration article 64 and seasonal certificate article 50 have different field lists. Explain the selected instrument's actual conditions.

The continuation paragraph omits the most useful comparison: article 21 runs to finality of the next permit decision, whereas article 71 runs to permit issuance or service of refusal. Seasonal work has several distinct pathways, including the omitted article 51 post-arrival pathway. "Until the case ends" is too imprecise. A form-filing receipt establishes an event; it does not by itself establish formal completeness, timely curing or every substantive condition.

The generic evidence labels such as "Дати чинності" are facts to establish, not documents. Name the actual prior permit/entry, contract, filed next application, submission proof, refusal decision and proof of service. A working comparison table is a working record, not a certificate issued by an authority.

## Proposed completed comparison

Use a new, clearly fictional paired comparison completed on 09.09.2026, distinct from 107's July-start/end notices. A Polish direct employer has two adult Moldovan employees in its own nonseasonal warehouse, on the same unchanged type of work. Their lawful stay on compatible Polish work-purpose visas remains independently valid through 31.12.2026. Neither has a separate status exemption, agency assignment or another work authorization. No permit revocation, party-requested suspension or separate work prohibition is part of the example.

Employee X worked under an ordinary permit valid 01.05–31.08.2026. Employee Y worked under a declaration registered in April for 01.05–31.08.2026 and actually held an employment contract throughout that period. On 20 August the employer filed separate formally complete permit applications for continuing the same positions and contracts. Keep the previous conditions unchanged; do not introduce a salary threshold calculation. The prior permit/entry and filing histories are expressly current-law 2026 documents.

Both next-permit refusals were issued 04.09 and delivered to the employer on 07.09. On 09.09 the employer filed timely appeals and completed its work-allocation note. Neither decision was final then; no appeal waiver or later appellate decision existed. X continued the previously authorized work under article 21. The employer withheld Y's work from 08.09 because article 71's refusal-service endpoint had passed, despite an appeal being available. The example establishes this bounded difference on the recorded date, not a favorable appeal result or automatic employment termination. Refusal grounds need not be invented or accepted on their merits.
