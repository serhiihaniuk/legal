# KRAZ entry and agency service scope

Preparation on 12.09.2026. The legal-explanatory-writing skill applies. This batch addresses the missing document destination for KRAZ in the temporary-work and outsourcing articles. Its proposed identity is `employment-agency-register`, not a newly issued certificate.

## Reader question

What does an agency's current registry record show, how is it obtained, and what does it leave unresolved about a proposed referral? The guide must distinguish the registered entity, service type, conditional foreign-worker scope, dates and status. A historical certificate, a saved current view and an earlier annual activity report are different records.

## Primary source findings

[The compiled statute, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 620](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/620/text/U/D20250620Lj.pdf), preparation date 17.06.2026, was checked in the following scope. Physical page numbers are one-based.

- Pages 260–261, article 305: mediation and temporary-work services are distinct. The special scope concerns foreigners who require the specified permit or declaration. It requires two years since registration and provision of the relevant services, followed by a changed entry. It is not a restriction concerning every foreigner regardless of status.
- Pages 264–265, articles 308–309: the marshal keeps the public register. Identity, service type, registration date and the date of the specified foreign-worker services are separate data. A company's KRS/CEIDG identity does not establish its agency service scope.
- Pages 267–269, articles 310, 312–313: entry and changes have separate procedures. Article 312(2) permits commencement in a qualified delayed-entry situation after 21 days. An empty search result alone must not be presented as conclusive proof of unlawful activity. Elapsed years alone do not amend a service scope.
- Pages 270–272, articles 315–317: removal follows the relevant final decision. A prohibition under article 317 is immediately enforceable before subsequent removal. A visible record therefore does not negate an operative prohibition.
- Page 274, article 322: changes, suspension and resumption are distinct events; their notices have specified deadlines. Suspension is not automatically deletion.
- Pages 376–377, article 445: specified old mediation/temporary-work certificates survive until removal. Old personnel/vocational-advice certificates have a different rule. The foreign-service transition requires prior registration and the specified actual activity before 01.06.2025, with an application for changed scope within three months. Company age alone is insufficient.

Independent source review checked ELI status and the amendment chain. The compiled text includes the listed amendments through 2026 poz. 734. Future provisions of 2026 poz. 507 effective 14.10.2026 and 2026 poz. 1123 effective 01.01.2028 are not presented as current rules. This is a scoped review, not acceptance of the entire act.

[WUP Gdańsk's explanation of the reform](https://wupgdansk.praca.gov.pl/wydarzenia-kraz/-/asset_publisher/N3QCJbOAwzog/content/nowe-regulacje-prawne-w-zakresie-dzialalnosci-agencji-zatrudnienia), in the section about existing entries, explicitly states that routine certificate issuance was abolished and distinguishes old certificate types. Only the relevant human-authored paragraphs are relied on; the page's AI summary and unrelated local payment instructions are not authority for this guide. WUP Warszawa's September 2025 bulletin, page 10, independently confirms the certificate change.
