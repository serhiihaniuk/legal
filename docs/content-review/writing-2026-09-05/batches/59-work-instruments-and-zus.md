# Batch 59: employment articles and the resident document pilot

## Reader question and completed change

A case reader could not tell which concrete ZUS or income document was needed, or whether a repeated mention meant another submission. The resident pilot now has an open list in each of its six stages. Each occurrence names the document, an explicit prepare/submit/review/update action and a stage-specific explanation. Detailed document guides remain linked. Other case presentations remain unchanged pending the user's review of this pilot.

The resident register replaces the income umbrella with employer and tax certificates, a personal ZUS history, a current eZUS health-registration confirmation, a targeted bank statement and a monthly payroll calculation. These are selected evidence for the fictional employed applicant. The filing explanation still distinguishes statutory initial attachments from evidence of substantive conditions. The July statement answers the specific June-pay discrepancy; it is not a universal bank-history requirement. The payslip is first reviewed internally and then submitted with the response. Repeated passport, contract and certificate uses explain review or presentation of an original rather than silently demanding another copy.

Eight concrete document guides were added: ZUS insurance history, current health registration, payer no-arrears certificate, RCA, employer income certificate, tax income certificate, bank statement and payroll statement. They include completed fictional fragments and obtaining/reading instructions. The legacy ZUS umbrella is retained for unreviewed routes. The separate all-case audit records remaining ambiguous groups; its findings are not presented as already fixed.

The previously in-progress work-instruments and work-permit map articles were also completed. Both have detailed explanations and finished examples. The next map article remains work-entry.
