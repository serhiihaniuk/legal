## Primary sources checked

All checks are scoped to 12.09.2026. Page numbers below are physical PDF pages counted from 1.

1. [KPA consolidated text, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1691](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1691/text/T/D20251691L.pdf), opened live. The T PDF has 46 pages and compilation header 04.12.2025.
   - Pages 2–3, articles 1–5 read in full. Article 1 has six categories, including the legally entrusted other bodies under pkt 2. Article 2 separately includes Dział VIII. Article 2a concerns data-information obligations, not a new residence instrument. Article 3's exclusions have exceptions; § 4 expressly names § 1, § 2 and § 3 pkt 2, not organizational subordination under § 3 pkt 1. Article 4 concerns immunities and international agreements/custom, not a general lex-specialis formula. Article 5 § 1 supplies the general referral rule before § 2's definitions.
   - Page 24, article 104 § 1–2 read in full. A decision resolves merits wholly/partly or otherwise ends the case in that instance. No assumption that every decision grants a requested right.
   - Page 27, article 122a § 1–2 read in full, with adjacent 122b–122f inspected for boundaries. The enabling special provision is required before the mechanism of silence can apply. The module does not introduce an actual silent residence route, a calculated silence deadline or a certificate of silence.
   - Pages 39–40, articles 217–219 read in full. The two bases under 217 § 2 are alternative; a claimed legal interest must actually be established. Article 218 § 1's record-based issuance duty is specifically tied to 217 § 2 pkt 2. Necessary inquiry under 218 § 2 and refusal through an appealable postanowienie under 219 are distinguished. No seven-day deadline is taught as universal for all documents named zaświadczenie.
   - Pages 40–41, articles 221–227 read for the scope and meaning of Dział VIII. The module relies on 222's content rule and 227's complaint subject. No filing-channel or formal-requirement waiver is inferred from 222.
   - Page 42, articles 233–238 read in full; page 43, articles 239–241 read for the surrounding limits. A party's complaint concerning a not-yet-proceeding individual matter may trigger the statutory initiation consequence, while another person's complaint has its qualified ex-officio role. Pending-case complaints are handled within that case. A final-decision complaint follows its content and the applicable extraordinary conditions. Article 237 § 3/238 notification is not a new permit. Article 240 preserves the proper special procedure where applicable; article 241 identifies organizational improvement requests. No specific complaint authority, receipt, deadline or extraordinary outcome is invented.
2. [Base KPA ELI API](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/1960/168), obtained live through PowerShell `Invoke-RestMethod`. The latest listed amendment remains DU/2025/769, effective 13.07.2025; no later amendment is listed in that response. This is a metadata observation at the check date, not a future guarantee or complete commentary review.

## Completed comparison and its limits

The example uses four independent fictional drafts available on 09.09.2026. On 10.09, their preparer finishes a written classification. It is a completed documentary comparison, not a claim that an authority processed the requests.

- The new temporary-residence permit request seeks an individual determination. Its one-sentence fragment is not a complete form or a selected filing channel.
- The request to confirm the issue date and validity period of a permit granted by a decision dated 12.05.2026 seeks existing information. Its stated need in another proceeding does not establish the precise legal interest or prove the authority holds the relevant records. It remains correctly classified as a certificate request without a fabricated issuance outcome.
- The complaint concerns service at an information desk on 07.09.2026. The facts expressly exclude disagreement with the handling or outcome of an individual case. No competent complaint recipient or finding of misconduct is selected.
- The request to display current opening hours is an organizational improvement proposal. Calling it wniosek does not make it identical to the permit request.

The Polish four-row table is the preparer's illustrative working record. No document is submitted, no receipt exists, no certificate or new permit is issued, and no organizational improvement is promised. The completed result is four distinct identified subjects and separate drafts.
