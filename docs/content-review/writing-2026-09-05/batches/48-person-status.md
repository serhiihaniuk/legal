# Batch 48: identifying the person and applicable residence regime

Rewrote /map/person-status as a standalone article. It distinguishes identity, citizenship, residence status and proof of that status. It explains why the country issuing a travel document need not establish nationality, why confirmed Polish citizenship changes the classification, and why a non-EU spouse can fall under the EU-family regime.

The completed example follows a Ukrainian spouse of a German citizen who actually works in Poland. A filled five-row record connects each established fact to its evidence and consequence. The reasoning corrects an intake classification based only on the applicant's passport. It also identifies the precise missing facts if the file contains only passports and a marriage document. Neither missing proof nor a generic family relationship is treated as a definitive residence decision.

The page stays in the current-status chapter. Entry dates, work permissions and detailed UKR conditions remain in their own topics. Existing passport, residence-card and status-document guides are linked; their specimens are not duplicated. The authored article and its table use the shared renderer and responsive document layout. No renderer or schema changes were needed.
