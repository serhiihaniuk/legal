# Two-instance review and KPA overview: preparation

Prepared on 12.09.2026 during batch93. Ohm read the complete legacy principle-two-instance.ts and kpa-principles.ts and checked the neighboring appeal, decision-appeal and extraordinary-wsa articles for duplication boundaries. This is preparation, not acceptance of either pending article.

The two-instance article should explain administrative re-examination of the case, ostateczność and prawomocność, exceptions and waiver, and the transition to judicial review. The legacy text names the two statuses without defining them. Add the qualified art. 127 § 1a exception: reasons were omitted because the party's request was fully granted. Explain same-organ reconsideration under art. 127 § 3 separately from an appeal to a higher authority. Art. 127a makes receipt of the waiver from the last party decisive and gives the decision both statuses. Do not imply ordinary judicial review remains available after every route to finality.

A completed comparison of dated decision records would be more useful here than another appeal letter. Distinguish an ordinary decision upheld on appeal, a decision satisfying art. 127 § 1a, and a waiver received from the last party. State the resulting procedural position without inventing a court judgment. The existing appeal and judicial-complaint guides own submission details. A remand under KPA art. 138 § 2 has a special sprzeciw route; do not describe every judicial challenge as a 30-day skarga.

The KPA overview should explain how principles govern concrete actions and cover the relevant principles beyond its child articles. Correct the legacy use of правомірність for prawomocność and невідворотну for irreparable harm. Avoid a universal prejudice test: the requirements depend on the remedy. Finish the overview after its children form a coherent set of articles.
