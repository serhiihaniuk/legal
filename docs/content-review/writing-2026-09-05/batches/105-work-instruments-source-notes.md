## Authoring decisions and limits

Temporary agency work belongs to article 6 ust. 1 pkt 1, not a fifth permit category. Corporate work means the named functions, not share ownership alone. Posting requires the foreign employer and actual foreign work; the third article 40 ust. 1 model excludes both the named host model and services. An export service is temporary and occasional, performed by a foreign entity that does not conduct business activity in Poland within the incorporated statutory meaning. No universal workplace or contract-type field is imported into article 42.

The seasonal permit is a decision. The application-registration certificate and arrival statement have different roles. Article 51 ust. 3 permits work on certificate conditions from submission of the required post-arrival documents to service of the decision, excluding party-requested suspension periods. The paragraph explains the separate next-office-working-day rule in ust. 4. It does not give every initial permit receipt the same effect.

Declaration nationality, Polish employer and contract, work in Poland, nonseasonal activity, noncorporate function, maximum 24-month period and start within six months are explained together. The example already has a registered entry. The article does not say that these entry conditions guarantee registration or resolve all refusal grounds. The continuation paragraph distinguishes existing work from a first application without reproducing the complete article 21 or 71 test.

Polish permanent residence supplies an independent basis. Karta Polaka and the selected Polish-university graduate exemption retain the opening stay condition in article 3 ust. 5, including separate visa-free wording. Student status is not substituted for graduation. The EEA employee-posting exemption retains lawful residence, employment within a stosunek pracy, usual foreign work and temporary posting by that same employer. Scope exclusion is illustrated separately by article 1 ust. 4 pkt 6 lit. a and the CEIDG/civil-contract/business combination in pkt 1. A B2B label alone is insufficient.

Initial article 5a notification is distinguished from the protection-based right. The module does not generalize repeated-trigger wording in ust. 5 pkt 1, which specifically names a Ukrainian citizen, to all protected nationalities. Article 70 notifications are a separate mechanism. There are no claims about authenticated portal controls, launch of every technical function, an actual submission by this author, or a universal new notification for every already working Ukrainian citizen.

- I is Indian. A submitted its first permit application on 3 August. There is an application and filing proof, but no issued permit as of 31 August. Lawful stay on a compatible work visa is stipulated; there is no other work basis or continuation route. A stopped the planned admission on 31 August. I did not begin on 1 September. No official refusal is invented.
- P is Indian with a current Polish permanent-residence permit, established through the decision and current card. A corrected its internal claim that every Indian national must await a work permit. The result rests on P's status, not on A's application or the declaration nationality list.
