## Completed fictional comparison

The distinct ordinary 2026 KPA proceeding concerns a witness's direct observation of the handover of a completed inventory report on 1 June 2026. Relevance to the proceeding is stipulated. The witness currently lives and stays in Gdańsk; the conducting organ is in Wrocław. The witness is neither the party nor its representative or an expert.

- 25 August: proper service of a summons to personal witness examination in Wrocław on 10 September.
- 27 August: signed venue request actually submitted and its receipt retained. It does not supply testimony or cancel the appointment.
- 31 August: the conducting organ determines that the nature of the case/action does not require examination before it, cancels the original appointment and requests local assistance with the factual question specified.
- 2 September: separate proper service of the local witness summons and notices to every party for 14 September. This gives more than the seven-day minimum for party notice under 79.
- 14 September: actual local examination after the proper warnings, direct observation separated from hearsay, protocol read/signed and forwarded to the conducting organ.
