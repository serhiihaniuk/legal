# Business case and corporate work permit

## Question and completed work

The learner needs to distinguish ownership from corporate work, choose the appropriate residence basis, prove the company's economic condition, answer a request for evidence and read the limits of the resulting permit.

Read the complete replacement business guide against `legal-explanatory-writing`. Its six stable stages follow one fictional Indian applicant who holds 40% of a spółka z o.o. and works in its zarząd. The new application is filed on 10 August 2026 during lawful stay. An existing corporate work permit covers the current function. The case is expressly outside UKR and earlier paper proceedings.

- The company relies on employment. Two Polish citizens have continuous full-time contracts of indefinite duration for the required year. A B2B contractor is excluded. The first packet contains only three months of one employee's reports. Additional records confirm an existing historical fact; a new certificate could not create missing months of employment.
- The representative receives a fourteen-day evidence request on 18 August. The reply on 28 August precedes the 1 September deadline. Filing proof, evidence sufficiency, personal appearance and the authority's processing period remain separate.
- Personal remuneration, insurance and housing are checked separately from company money, employee contributions and an office lease. The alternatives explain a new business, a board member without shares, other refusal grounds and ended activity.
- The route uses 36 shared document entries at their relevant stages. Corporate annexes, CRBR, employee contracts, work permits and professional qualifications retain their conditional scope. The ordinary business stamp duty is 340 zł; the card fee is separate.
- Overview, document definitions, stages and references are separate authored modules. The twenty common temporary-residence definitions are reused with the business fee override. Six conditions, seven deadline entries and four alternatives remain in the existing folded reference sections.

The business-evidence guide retains its completed startup example and adds a filled three-row employee comparison matching this case. A new sixtieth document guide explains the corporate work permit, including a selected fictional Polish permit fragment and the completed conclusion that a permit for company A does not automatically cover company B. The fragment is not an official form, complete decision or client document.

## Official checks and date limits

Checks are dated 6 September 2026. Exact passages were read in official texts or in official reading PDFs retained from the preceding batches. ELI status and current reading-text links were checked. Common procedure and document requirements also use the source readings recorded in batches 07, 14, 15 and 17.

- [Foreigners act, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1079](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1079/ogl), official reading PDF dated 7 April 2026, including the listed later amendments. Read art. 142–143a, the comparison with art. 126, personal-income reference to art. 140 ust. 2, corporate refusal references to art. 117 pkt 1–2 and the corporate annex in art. 106 ust. 2. The residence economic test offers income or qualifying employment, with a separate future-performance alternative. The employment categories refer to the specified provisions of the current work act. The text distinguishes an enabling provision for limits from proof that a limit has been imposed or reached. Common filing and notification provisions retain their separate roles.
- [Work act, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 621](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/621/ogl), official reading PDF dated 17 June 2026. Read art. 3 and art. 35–39. Corporate work-permit art. 36 ust. 2 combines income and employment; its future-performance alternative is in ust. 3. The referenced worker categories and GUS timing differ from the residence test. Art. 37 gives the permit fields, art. 38 the specified one-year limit, and art. 39 the conditional six-month exemption. The guide explains reading an existing permit, not the complete process or fees for obtaining one.
- [MOS amendment, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1794](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1794/ogl), especially art. 12 and 17, with the [commencement announcement, M.P. 2026 poz. 370](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/MP/2026/370/ogl). The new ordinary August application uses the procedure effective on 27 April 2026. The [application regulation, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 553](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/553/ogl) remains the original-form source checked in preceding document batches.
- [Special-rule changes, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/203/ogl), art. 45. Its listed temporary-protection exception and biometric rules are not described as an economic waiver. The current special-act art. 100d reading from batch 15 supports the qualification concerning specified authority periods through 4 March 2027. It does not extend an applicant's evidence-response deadline.
- [Stamp duty, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1154](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1154/ogl), official reading text dated 3 August 2026, annex III item 2. Business residence uses the ordinary 340 zł rate, with the stated temporary-protection exemption. [Card fees, Dz.U. 2022 poz. 1583](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2022/1583/ogl), §§ 2 and 4, support the separate 100 zł standard and proof of a reduction. ELI still identifies this regulation as in force.
- [Podlaski UW business guidance](https://www.gov.pl/web/uw-podlaski/dzialalnosc-gospodarcza) supplies supporting examples of financial, employment and investment materials. Its mixed paper-era instructions are not used for the new MOS filing procedure.
- [KPA, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1691](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/1691/ogl), with the common readings recorded in preceding batches: representation, delivery, art. 57 deadline calculation, restoration, evidence and file access, and art. 129 appeals.
