## Corrections and case integration

The university form is the fifth annex to the application and the sixth annex in the forms regulation. The guide explains both numbers. The new MOS procedure starts on 27.04.2026; earlier unfinished proceedings retain their earlier rules. A university signature and the applicant's completed filing are separate events.

The certificate is not described as a second universally mandatory copy of the MOS annex. The progress guide distinguishes the current annex's progress fields from the older certificate-and-transcript requirement. It preserves the difference between possible refusal after removal from the student list and mandatory refusal where a previous student permit was used for another purpose. A failed subject alone is not presented as either finding.

The tuition guide separates a charge, a completed transfer and the university's allocation. Instalments, partial waivers and scholarships require their own evidence; they do not automatically establish full payment or free study. The certificate and progress pages define study forms and ECTS without inferring the form from weekend classes.

The translation guide separates the ordinary sworn-Polish-translation requirement for foreign-language evidence from the language rule before the foreign minister or consul. For work contracts, it distinguishes the understandable written version given before signing a Polish contract from the employer's duty to retain a sworn Polish translation of a contract executed in a foreign language. It explains repertorium, the translator-list number, source copies and electronic certification.

The apostille guide starts with possible exemptions and the two countries involved. The Polish-Ukrainian treaty exemption is limited to properly issued or authenticated documents of a competent authority. It is not extended to every file in Ukrainian. Authentication, translation and recognition of qualifications remain separate questions.

The student case now reuses four typed document definitions across qualification, filing, evidence and its register. Fifteen repeated objects were replaced. The annex is required for a new MOS application; certificates and separate progress records retain their conditional or earlier-procedure roles. Tuition evidence is tied to paid study. The earlier-procedure reference links to the amending act rather than attributing the transition to the annex-content provision. This is a focused integration correction. The complete student case remains pending writing and coverage review.
