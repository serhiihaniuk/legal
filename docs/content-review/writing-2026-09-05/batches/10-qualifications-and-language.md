## Corrections and case integration

For an unregulated profession, the qualification guide separates education from comparable experience. The education route requires a completed higher-education programme of at least three years at a recognised institution. Experience requires three years within the preceding seven years for the published professions, or five years outside that list. The guide relates the experience to the actual contracted work and explains why overlapping employment periods do not double the calendar time. A regulated profession needs its own formal qualifications; translation, apostille and nostrification have different roles.

The job-description guide no longer implies that a separately named file is mandatory in every application. It explains actual tasks, requirements and conflicting records. An employer's description of the role does not establish the applicant's qualifications.

The applicant's Blue Card annex is application annex 2 and regulation annex 3. The guide explains both numbers, the different author of the employer annex, and the new MOS procedure from 27.04.2026. Earlier unfinished proceedings retain the earlier rules. Parts IV and V concern different mobility histories; their 12-month and 6-month periods are not cumulative requirements for every applicant. The annex's signature and submission of the complete application remain separate events.

The language guide identifies permitted certificate categories, eligible university confirmations, completed Polish-medium education and the under-16 exemption. It does not turn a private course attendance record into an accepted certificate. For policealna evidence, it distinguishes proceedings already pending before 01.07.2025, proceedings begun through 30.06.2026 using a certificate of completion by 30.06.2025, and later new proceedings. Other qualifying education routes remain available.

The Blue Card case reuses three typed definitions for qualification evidence, a conditional separate job description and the applicant annex. The resident case reuses a language-evidence definition with the age condition and the transition reference. These replace 15 repeated objects. Focused Blue Card profile wording also now treats education and relevant experience as alternative methods. These edits do not constitute acceptance of either complete case route.
