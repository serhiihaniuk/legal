# Batch 45: KPA and special rules

Rewrote /map/special-vs-kpa as a standalone article that separates the conditions of a permit, the procedure for establishing them and the rules applicable to an older proceeding. The article explains that lex specialis resolves a particular question; it does not automatically displace all of KPA or identify a higher-ranking act.

A completed employer example contains a filled three-row note. It separates permit conditions, the special definition of a party and the continuing duty to evaluate evidence. A second example concerns an application received before MOS launched. Both conclusions remain bounded. The ICT comparison distinguishes granting a permit from withdrawing it. A short consular boundary prevents readers from applying the wojewoda example to every proceeding involving a foreigner.
