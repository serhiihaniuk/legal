## Completed institutional-record comparison

The invented proceeding began in June 2026. The party is in Poland, acts personally and has access to the selected record. The selected relevant fact is the issue date of a decision from another authority. That earlier decision is dated 14.01.2025 and remains unchanged.

- The institution's letter dated 03.08.2026 reaches the deciding authority on 4 August and incorrectly states 14.01.2026.
- On 5 August the party receives information about the new material and an opportunity to comment by 14 August. This is the fictional letter's selected date, not a general KPA minimum.
- On 6 August the party reads the letter in the case file. On 7 August it submits a signed explanation and a copy of the earlier decision, presents the original and requests certification. An authorised employee actually certifies copy conformity under article 76a(2b).
- The deciding authority requests clarification. The institution's reply dated 10 August, received on 11 August, confirms its transcription error. It corrects its information letter, not the earlier decision.
- On 12 August the authority assesses the decision, party's explanation and institutional clarification together and communicates the limited finding: the issue date is 14.01.2025. The current proceeding has not ended in a decision. The example does not cut short the comment opportunity ending on 14 August.

The causal sequence is completed: participation exposes a precise conflict; the submitted document makes verification possible; cooperation obtains the issuer's clarification; joint assessment removes the wrong year from the factual basis. Neither the party's objection alone nor an institutional letter's origin establishes its correctness. The working comparison records this sequence but is not itself proof of filing or receipt.

No real identifiers, residence entitlement, work right, computed appeal deadline, finality date or judicial outcome is invented. The specimen is a labelled preparer's comparison, not an official form or a new required attachment. Dates of issue, receipt, viewing, submission and assessment retain their separate meanings.
