## Entry-specific rewrite scopes

### Article 1: what sort of proceeding is included?

The existing summary lists most categories accurately, but its two rules compress six different statutory points. Its case paragraph repeats the generic material-law versus procedure distinction. Explain an individual matter within the authority's competence and the legally entrusted role of other bodies under pkt 2. Define an administrative decision as the relevant form of determination without promising that every office letter is a decision.

Separate pkt 1–2 from the additional proceedings under pkt 3–6. Preserve all six categories, including relief in the execution of administrative monetary penalties, which the current summary omits. Distinguish a dispute over competence from an ordinary disagreement with the outcome. Keep penalty and European cooperation explanations at orientation level.

The silent-settlement reference needs the adjacent qualification from article 122a § 1: a special provision must enable it. An unexpired or expired office response period alone is not that enabling provision. Do not use the opening list as a universal residence grant rule.

Suggested completed micro-example for `foreignersCase`: an illustrative document is headed `Decyzja` and its selected operative sentence grants a named residence permission. A staff member correctly identifies an individual determination, while a separate confirmation of an existing recorded fact is identified as a certificate matter. State what those two classifications establish. Do not decide the certificate's eligibility or invent its issuance. Keep this to one paragraph rather than repeating the system module's full comparison.

### Article 2: what does skarga or wniosek mean here?

The existing entry identifies Dział VIII but leaves the reader with only the warning that a complaint does not replace an appeal. Explain the subject of this complaint/request procedure and distinguish an organizational proposal from an application for an individual entitlement. Article 222 makes the content decisive for the skarga/wniosek classification. It does not erase filing requirements or determine every available remedy.

Preserve the statutory bodies named in article 2. Avoid implying this is a general complaints procedure against every private business. Where the content concerns an individual case, explain briefly that articles 233–235 depend on whether proceedings have not started, are pending or have ended in a final decision. Do not describe Dział VIII as an independent substitute regardless of that stage.

Suggested completed micro-example: two illustrative fragments ask respectively to examine a clerk's conduct at an information desk and to improve the posted service hours. The first concerns conduct, the second organization. The fictional facts expressly exclude disagreement with a pending or final individual decision. Record the completed distinction, not a promise about the competent complaint recipient or response deadline. A separate sentence can explain why an objection to a permit decision needs its procedural remedy examined.

### Article 2a: which RODO obligations, and what does not stop?

Correct the visible doubled `RODO RODO` in the summary and the broad final phrase "вимога за RODO". The provision names the information obligation under RODO article 13 ust. 1 and 2 and, separately, a request under article 18 ust. 1. It does not say that every privacy request has the same legal consequences.

Use separate rules for KPA § 1, § 2 and § 3. Explain what information about processing is, how it differs from explanations about the merits under KPA, and why these duties coexist. The statement about the course and outcome of the proceeding does not excuse defective information or inaccurate evidence. Article 2a is not itself a blanket authorization for any processing.

For a concrete procedural connection, use one exact KPA rule: article 61 § 5 or article 54 § 1a, each retaining its existing-information qualification. Do not attribute one universal delivery moment to article 2a. Article 217a and article 226a show other procedural placements; the latter's text must not be silently given the former's exception. Two well-explained connections are enough.

Suggested completed micro-example: on 3 September a party disputes the accuracy of a recorded address and requests restriction of processing. On 4 September the worker separates the privacy request from the substantive case correspondence and corrects a working note which had said the case must now stop. The completed result is that classification and correction. Do not invent acceptance of the RODO request, automatic correction of an official record, a suspended deadline or a permit result. Keep the claim about non-interruption tied to the specified request, with an explicit statement that handling the data issue remains separate.

### Article 3: exclusions must be read with the retained rules

The current phrase "більшості податкових справ" should be replaced by the actual boundary: matters governed by `Ordynacja podatkowa`, with the express retention of Dział IV and VIII. Do not erase the prosecutor-participation exception simply because complaints are more familiar.

Give separate, readable rules for § 1, § 2, § 3, § 4 and § 5. Explain `karne skarbowe` as the named penal-fiscal category rather than all criminal matters. For diplomatic and consular matters, keep the special-provision qualification beside the exclusion. Distinguish organizational subordination between the named bodies/entities from the service subordination of their employees.

The exact § 4 list is § 1, § 2 and § 3 pkt 2. It does not include § 3 pkt 1. A generic sentence saying that Dział VIII survives "for excluded cases" hides this difference. Section 5 authorizes extension by regulation to § 2 proceedings; it does not itself extend the code to every such matter or all of § 3.

Suggested completed micro-example: a draft note about a tax matter governed by Ordynacja says `KPA nie stosuje się w żadnym zakresie`. The reviewer replaces it with the exact limited conclusion, retaining Dział IV and VIII. This demonstrates a real correction without inventing a tax assessment or a complaint outcome. The foreigner relevance can add that receiving one paper from a consul does not justify importing the whole ordinary KPA residence procedure. An operational consular remedy needs its own freshly checked special source and is outside this batch.

### Article 4: preservation of special rights, not an invented hierarchy

The current rule is short, but the phrase "пріоритетне значення" in its effect suggests a general rule of hierarchy which article 4 does not formulate. Explain the operative meaning of `nie narusza`: applying KPA does not remove the special rights arising from the specified immunities, international agreements and customs.

Do not reduce agreements and customs to diplomatic immunity alone. Conversely, do not imply that every foreigner, every consular document or every international element supplies a special right. The right and its applicable source must be identified. Article 4 does not establish the person's diplomatic status, list all immunities or confer a blanket exemption from administrative proceedings.

No elaborate fictional immunity decision is needed. A concise bounded illustration can say that a foreign passport by itself does not identify the particular special right preserved by article 4. Explain what remains missing without manufacturing a Vienna Convention or bilateral-treaty conclusion. A real asserted privilege would require checking the specific treaty, status and exception, which this preparation has not done.

### Article 5: a referral rule and defined terms

Keep § 1 separate from the dictionary. The statutory condition is a provision of law referring generally to provisions on administrative procedure. An office's informal use of that phrase does not itself create a statutory referral or competence.

Unpack the active § 2 points rather than saying only that definitions exist. Explain that `organ administracji publicznej` includes the named bodies and legally entrusted entities; `minister` has a code-specific scope wider than an everyday cabinet job title. Give concrete named items from pkt 3 and 6, such as wojewoda and starosta, without claiming this determines which one handles a particular permit. Distinguish an organ from the office supporting it. Preserve pkt 2 as repealed if the structure is displayed, without inventing replacement content.

The present effect adds "якщо контекст норми не вимагає іншого висновку" without support from this article. Remove that free-standing escape clause. Do not imply that every social organization automatically has powers to decide individual matters merely because pkt 5 defines the term.

Suggested completed micro-example: an illustrative paper names `Wojewoda` as the organ but carries the supporting office's postal address. A working note is corrected to identify the organ separately from the office. The completed result identifies the actor; it does not resolve territorial competence, authorization to sign or an appeal route. A short second example can interpret a deliberately labelled hypothetical statutory referral under § 1, never presented as a quotation from an actual special law.

## Fit within the current fields

No large schema addition is required for these six entries. Use `summary` for the article's central answer; `rules` for clause-specific explanations; `legalEffect` for the consequence and immediate limit; and `foreignersCase` for a short completed application or illustration. A few connected sentences can contain the facts, interpretation and bounded result. Not every article needs the same number of rules or a dated persona.

The contract accepts `LegalTextValue`, not block sections or a `DocumentSample`. The generic route and embedded module renderer place each explanation inside a paragraph. Markdown tables, multiline letters, nested paragraph markup or a sample object inserted into these fields will not become a proper specimen. Use short selected Polish phrases inside normal text. A future need for a substantial document sample would justify a separate bounded schema discussion, not a workaround in this batch.

Use typed KPA references for additional provisions and an explicit external RODO source. A bare trailing article number must not imply its act. The external link can carry the exact article/paragraph locator; the existing KPA entry wrapper has no separate per-entry source-list field. Its official ELI source identifies the KPA edition, not an independently verified RODO rule. Source claims should stay close to their supporting references. Do not force a document-guide link where no specific document is being explained.
