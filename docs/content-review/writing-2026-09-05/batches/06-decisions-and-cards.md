## Corrections and case integration

The decision guide separates the outcome, reasons and appeal instructions. It explains the statutory exceptions to reasons, administrative finality, judicial finality, waiver of appeal and enforceability. It does not imply that every favorable first-instance decision requires a fourteen-day wait. It distinguishes a document missing from the file from a document that the authority received but found insufficient for the relevant period.

The card guide replaces a circular description of the card with its statutory identity and travel functions. It distinguishes card validity from the indefinite permanent-residence and EU long-term-resident permits, and the work-access annotation from the specific decision conditions. The employer example states that no alternative authorization or exemption was established. Collection and replacement are explained separately from granting or changing a permit.

The fee guide separates the permit's stamp fee from the card payment, including their recipients. It explains the standard amount, fifty-percent reductions, exemptions, higher fees for culpable loss, and the applicable supporting documents. It preserves the older-application transition and explains UdSC's initial MOS payment instructions.

Four work-case rows now share one card-payment definition. The row is conditional on payment being due, identifies 100 zł as the standard amount, retains the typed document reference and explains checking an existing payment at collection. The complete work-case guide remains pending review; these focused corrections do not accept its other requirements.
