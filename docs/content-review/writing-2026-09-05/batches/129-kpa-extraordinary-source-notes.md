## Completed example and evidence roles

The example is expressly a simplified general KPA hypothetical. Its assumed substantive condition concerns a qualification at an earlier relevant date. It does not teach a real professional licensing rule or a residence-permit qualification requirement. The statutory procedure, limits and identified dates are the subject of the explanation.

Selected chronology:

1. Original refusal issued 15 June 2020, duly served 18 June 2020 and later final without an appeal. No earlier extraordinary proceeding is assumed.
2. Existing 2019 examination protocol discovered by the party on 3 August 2026. Copy and archive covering letter obtained then. The original protocol existed before the decision, was unknown to the issuer and materially addressed the assumed qualification condition. The date of the covering letter does not become the date of the underlying qualification or old protocol.
3. Signed reopening request, protocol copy and archive letter actually accepted at the first-instance authority on 24 August 2026. The receipt on the retained copy confirms filing. The month under Article 148 ended on 3 September, calculated under Article 57 § 3–4.
4. Competent authority issued the opening order on 28 August. It was also the last-instance issuer; the selected ground does not concern its own activity in the sense of Article 150 § 2.
5. The investigation established the ground. A decision under Article 151 § 2 was issued 15 September and served 18 September. It declared that the old decision had been issued unlawfully and identified the five-year limit as preventing repeal. More than six years had elapsed since the stipulated 2020 service, so the example does not depend on an unresolved last-day boundary for that long limit.

The twelve Polish cells compare four stages: old decision, discovery/request, opening/findings and issued result. They are a completed private working comparison of separate documents. They are not a mandatory application form or a reproduced real decision. The two cells containing statutory locators use typed legal references. Finality of the new decision, a subsequent appeal, grant of entitlement and compensation are not asserted.

Existing typed document destinations are used only for their actual roles:

- `administrative-decision` explains the act and operative result, not the reopening request.
- `procedural-order` explains the opening order, without inventing a separate complaint against that order.
- `delivery-proof` supports the 2020 service event used for the limit and the distinct 2026 receipt of the new result.
- `dispatch-proof` supports actual filing, not proof of the merits or every enclosure.
- `case-file-index` is expressly a private working aid, not a mandatory attachment or replacement for evidence.

The signed reopening request, examination protocol and archive letter are named documents with no invented catalog destination. No existing ordinary-appeal, generic-response or graduation-diploma guide is misrepresented as their specific guide. No suspension request is added to the selected facts.
