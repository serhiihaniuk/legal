# Case-office deadlines

Reviewed on 06.09.2026. This is an amendment to the complete case reviews in batches 15–21 and 23, and the shared explanation in batch 24. The reader's question is whether the authority's suspended deadline changes their response deadline or prevents court review of delay.

## Changes

All eight ordinary residence guides now reuse a typed explanation of the statutory suspension, the applicant's separate deadlines and the distinction between administrative application and judicial review. Each procedure stage retains its own example dates and explanation of the ordinary period. Each deadline register uses the same qualification. CUKR issuance retains its separate rules.

The family and business registers now identify the ordinary 60-day period and its starting conditions, instead of presenting the suspension's end date as the whole deadline. Permanent residence and EU resident status retain their six-month periods. The EU resident register now names art. 100d explicitly in its source label.

The business and graduate registers incorrectly described missed required attendance as leaving the application unexamined. They now distinguish termination under art. 106i, including its conditions and exceptions, from the other procedural outcomes. Supporting financial or insurance evidence does not perform a separate required personal action.
