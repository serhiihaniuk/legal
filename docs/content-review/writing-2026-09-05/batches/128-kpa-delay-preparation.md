## Main findings

The ordinary KPA explanation is broadly qualified, but it does not give a completed administrative chronology showing the original period, a notified new date, actual filing of ponaglenie, receipt by the reviewing authority and the resulting order. Its two separate seven-day periods need a concrete example. The no-superior-authority mechanism is mentioned but not explained; it does not end in the same Article 37 § 6 order. Employee responsibility and money awarded by a court are different consequences.

Residence needs three layers that must remain visible beside each other:

1. The applicable special time limit and its starting events, including the post-MOS version and the transition for older proceedings.
2. The current statutory suspension and remedy restrictions in Article 100d, for its listed cases before a wojewoda.
3. The distinct judicial treatment described in official judgments and the ministry's response. This does not repeal the statutory text or guarantee that a particular complaint will succeed.

The shared text's current endpoint, 4 March 2027, is supported by the refreshed official sources. It correctly does not confine the list to Ukrainian nationals or automatically apply it to the appeal stage before Szef UdSC. The published Poznań judgment remains marked `nieprawomocne` on 23 September. Its reasons contain conflicting descriptions of the initial application/complaint dates. ## Sources and exact ordinary rules

All sources below were retrieved live on 23 September 2026. Physical PDF pages count from one. Some web-reader calls failed on PDF URLs; direct HTTPS retrieval of the official PDFs succeeded. No authenticated service or browser submission was used.

### KPA

[KPA, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1691, current text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1691/text/T/D20251691L.pdf), physical pages 8–9, Articles 35–38. Same-day [base-act metadata](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/1960/168) still lists 769, effective 13 July 2025, as the latest amendment. The current document includes that amendment. Its older-case transition is [Article 31(1), Dz.U. 2025 poz. 769](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/769/text/O/D20250769.pdf), physical page 19; commencement is Article 48, page 21. The proposed new June 2026 case avoids that transition.

- Article 35 §§ 1–2: without unnecessary delay, immediately where the case can be resolved on the specified already available facts/evidence. A maximum period is not permission to wait until its end without a need.
- Article 35 §§ 3–4: ordinary investigation within one month, particularly complicated case within two months from initiation; appeal within one month from receipt. Simplified proceedings have the separate immediate/no-later-than-one-month wording in § 3a. Special provisions can establish other periods.
- Article 35 § 5: exclude prescribed periods for identified acts, public hybrid-service delivery periods, suspension, mediation, delay attributable to the party and causes independent of the authority. Do not replace those facts with an unexplained subtraction of every period between letters. The legacy list uses 'including', so its omission of hybrid delivery is not itself a contradiction; the rewrite should state that actual exclusion if giving the full list.
- Article 36: notice of failure to conclude on time must state the reasons, new date and right to ponaglenie, including delay independent of the authority. A new date does not establish that the previous handling was necessary or lawful.
- Article 37 § 1: distinguish failure to conclude within the applicable statutory/notified period from proceedings taking longer than necessary. Under § 3a, a ponaglenie filed before the Article 35 or special-law period expires is left unexamined, with §§ 4–8 inapplicable. Do not bypass that express rule just by labelling the allegation `przewlekłość`.
- Article 37 §§ 2–5: reasons are required; route through the case authority to the superior, or to the case authority if no superior exists. Forwarding is without unnecessary delay and no later than seven days from receipt, with necessary copies and the authority's position. Review is within seven days from receipt by the reviewing authority. These are distinct receipt events, not a promise of an answer seven days after the party sends a letter.
- Article 37 § 6: the superior's order determines whether there was inactivity/protracted handling and whether it was a gross violation. If a violation is found and the case remains pending, it sets a completion date. It orders examination of causes and responsible persons and, if necessary, preventive measures. This is not an order to grant the requested substantive right.
- Article 37 § 7: a longer completion date in that order requires newly revealed important facts or evidence requiring longer proceedings, unknown when the date was set. It is not unrestricted renewal.
- Article 37 § 8: without a superior, §§ 4, 6 and 7 do not apply. If the case authority finds delay, it concludes the case without delay and orders the specified internal examination/measures. Do not describe a universal self-issued § 6 order.
- Article 38 concerns the employee's disciplinary, order-related or other statutory responsibility for unjustified delay. It does not itself award money to the applicant.

Supporting calculation rules are Article 57, physical page 14, particularly the month rule in § 3 and the endpoint rule in § 4. The proposed ordinary example expressly excludes periods requiring subtraction.

### PPSA

Use the [current compiled PPSA, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 143](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/143/text/U/D20260143Lj.pdf), 99 physical pages, dated 20 July 2026. Do not silently reuse page numbers from the shorter original `T` text. [Base-act metadata](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2002/1270) lists 846, effective 26 June 2026, as the latest amendment. [Dz.U. 2026 poz. 846](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/846/text/O/D20260846.pdf), Article 10 on page 25, changes Article 53 § 1 and Article 264 § 2, not the selected Article 53 § 2b rule.

- Article 53 § 2b and Article 54 § 1, physical page 24: inactivity/protracted-proceeding complaint may be lodged at any time after ponaglenie to the competent authority, through the authority whose conduct is challenged. Do not substitute a thirty-day decision-complaint period or a requirement to obtain a favourable ponaglenie result.
- Article 149, physical pages 58–59: keep the categories within its scope. The court may require the specified act/action or recognition of a right/duty and finds inactivity/protracted handling. It separately decides whether the violation was gross. Section 1b permits a ruling about the existence of a right/duty only where the nature of the case and the unambiguous factual/legal circumstances allow it. Do not state categorically that a court can never address a right.
- Article 149 § 2 permits a fine or a monetary sum for the complainant under the stated ceilings; these are not automatic consequences or the same as litigation costs. No numerical ceiling is needed in this module unless its separate calculation basis is verified.

## Residence: current statutory scope and dates

### Article 100d remains in force

[Current compiled Ukraine assistance Act, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 337](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/337/text/U/D20250337Lj.pdf), physical pages 57–58, text dated 22 May 2026. [Base-act metadata](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2022/583) lists 635 effective 27 May 2026 and 203 effective 5 March 2026 among the current amendments.

[Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/203/text/O/D20260203.pdf), Article 17 point 50 on physical page 33, changes Article 100d's endpoint from 4 March 2026 to 4 March 2027. Article 54 on page 42 makes this change effective 5 March 2026. It is not one of the deferred exceptions. Article 17 point 49 repeals Articles 96–100c, not Article 100d.

The later [Dz.U. 2026 poz. 635](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/635/text/O/D20260635.pdf), Article 18 on physical page 14, changes financial provisions of the assistance Act, not Article 100d. Its Article 26 on page 15 gives the fourteen-day commencement, corresponding to 27 May 2026.

The exact current Article 100d list covers proceedings before a wojewoda concerning:

- grant of temporary, permanent or long-term EU resident permits;
- change of temporary residence/work and temporary residence for highly qualified work permits;
- withdrawal of temporary, permanent or long-term EU resident permits.

Until 4 March 2027, the case-completion periods do not start, and started periods are suspended. The wording refers to foreigners, not only Ukrainian citizens. It identifies proceedings before the wojewoda; it does not itself suspend the appeal-stage period before Szef UdSC. It is not a general freeze of all administration, every immigration procedure, a work-permit proceeding or the applicant's reply/appeal period.

Paragraph 2 preserves the effectiveness of acts performed during the period. Paragraph 3 excludes the named inactivity/delay-notification provisions and the stated fines/monetary awards. Paragraph 4 bars deriving the named delay remedies from cessation or delayed acts in that period. These are the literal statutory restrictions. Do not describe the entire proceeding as suspended or infer that the authority may not issue a decision.

### Special residence clocks and MOS transition

[Current foreigners Act, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1079](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1079/text/U/D20251079Lj.pdf), dated 7 April 2026, prints bracketed old and launch-dependent new provisions together. Its typography alone does not establish which text applies on 23 September.

- Article 112a, physical pages 141–142: temporary grant ordinarily has a sixty-day period, with the specified latest-event starting rule. In the post-launch version, the old personal-appearance point in paragraph 2 is repealed. The remaining relevant events are a formally complete/cured application and the specified evidence submission or expiry of the evidence deadline under Article 106f. Paragraphs 3 and 3a provide their own host-entity and separated-family cases. Paragraphs 4–5 provide a ninety-day appeal period and its formal-defect cure qualification.
- Article 210, physical page 286: permanent grant has six months, again with the post-launch latest-event conditions and the repeal of the old appearance point. Paragraphs 3–4 provide ninety days for appeal, with the cure qualification.
- Article 222d, physical page 309: the new long-term EU resident provision directly states six months, latest formal/evidence events and ninety days for appeal with the cure qualification. Do not use repealed Article 223 as the current bridge to Article 210 for a new post-launch case.

The legal chain is [Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1794](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1794/text/O/D20251794.pdf), Article 12 and Article 17 on physical page 40, Article 19 on page 41, plus [M.P. 2026 poz. 370](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/MP/2026/370/text.pdf), its single page. The communiqué fixes 27 April 2026. Article 12 preserves prior rules for the specified already initiated, unfinished proceedings. The old court example concerns a 2025 application; do not replace its then-applicable appearance/evidence rule with the new version retrospectively.

These special clocks do not override Article 100d just because the reader has identified a nominal sixty-day or six-month period. The module should present both layers. A precise complete residence clock example would require selecting the type, filing regime, completion events, authority, excluded periods and period of alleged delay. The proposed main example below intentionally establishes the ordinary non-residence regime instead.

## Administration and judicial assessment must remain distinct

Read the actual [MSWiA response to RPO, 30 June 2026](https://bip.brpo.gov.pl/sites/default/files/2026-08/Odpowiedz_MSWiA_cudzoziemcy_legalizacja_pobytu_przewleklosc_30_06_2026.pdf), physical pages 7–9, particularly page 9. The ministry describes a high probability that courts will decline to apply Article 100d to delay after 30 June 2024, while Szef UdSC remains obliged to apply it administratively when reviewing ponaglenia. This is attributed official guidance about judicial practice, not repeal of Article 100d or a binding promise for a future litigant.

Reopened [WSA Poznań, 12 March 2026, II SAB/Po 314/25](https://orzeczenia.nsa.gov.pl/doc/1E120C483B). On 23 September, CBOSA still labels it `orzeczenie nieprawomocne`. The operative part requires the wojewoda to resolve the application within sixty days from receipt of the final judgment copy with the administrative file; finds inactivity but not a gross violation; rejects the remainder and awards litigation costs. It does not grant a permit or establish that the judgment has already been executed.

The reasons adopt the post-30-June-2024 constitutional distinction but describe the earlier statutory endpoint. That historical discussion is not evidence that the current statute still ends in March 2026. The reasons also contain inconsistent early application/complaint dates. Do not silently repair or harmonize those dates. A short attributed account of the verified operative result is supportable; a new date-by-date reconstruction is not proposed.

No comprehensive survey establishes that every court currently follows the same approach. No later finality event for this individual judgment was located. Those limits must remain explicit if the judicial example is retained.

## Proposed full article structure

1. **A deadline and unnecessary delay answer different questions.** Define inactivity/protracted handling and immediate handling, statutory maximums, special periods and identified exclusions.
2. **What a delay notice changes.** Article 36's actual contents, the difference between a new date and justification of past inactivity, and the record needed to identify the applicable period.
3. **What ponaglenie does.** Reasons, competent route, premature-filing rule, separate receipt clocks, superior's order and the distinct no-superior procedure. Explain the possible later extension and employee responsibility without turning either into a promised payment.
4. **Residence periods cannot be copied from ordinary KPA.** Current special clocks, post-MOS/older-case boundary, the exact Article 100d list and endpoint. Explain the continuing effectiveness of acts and independent applicant deadlines.
5. **Court review concerns a separate remedy and outcome.** Filing after ponaglenie, proper route, powers and qualifications under Article 149; literal statutory restriction versus attributed ministry/judicial treatment after 30 June 2024.
6. **Completed ordinary administrative record.** Use the fictional chronology below rather than requiring the reader to draft a complaint. The existing judgment may remain a shorter separate illustration within section five, with current checked status.

## Proposed completed fiction and Polish record

A new ordinary individual KPA proceeding begins on 3 June 2026 when a formally complete signed request reaches the competent authority. It needs an investigation but is not particularly complicated. A superior authority exists. This is not a residence or work-permit proceeding and has no special deadline. No suspension, mediation, hybrid delivery period, prescribed waiting period, party-caused delay or other excluded period occurs. Necessary evidence has been collected by 18 June. The statutory one-month endpoint is 3 July.

On 30 June the authority serves a proper Article 36 notice naming the unfinished evidence assessment and setting 17 July as the new completion date, with the required instruction. No decision is issued by that date. The case record contains no new evidence or necessary action explaining the continued wait.

The party physically files a signed, reasoned ponaglenie through the case authority on 20 July. The authority forwards it with the required material and position on 22 July; the superior receives it on 23 July. On 28 July the superior issues an order finding inactivity without a gross violation, requiring completion by 11 August because the case is still pending and ordering examination of causes/responsible persons. It does not order a grant. The case authority issues its substantive decision on 7 August and properly serves it on 10 August. The example records the completion and its dates, not whether the substantive result was correct. No court complaint or damages award is invented.

The dates let the reader see actual performance of both seven-day duties without treating them as one clock. The ponaglenie comes after both the original and notified periods, so the example does not require resolving a premature-filing dispute. The superior's stated finding is an expressly fictional completed act, not an assertion that every similar delay is necessarily non-gross.

## Document targets and dependencies

- No dedicated ponaglenie guide was found in the current catalog source scan. Name the actual signed request plainly with Article 37; do not route it to administrative-appeal, procedural-complaint or judicial-complaint.
- `procedural-order` fits the superior's issued Article 37 § 6 order, labelled with that specific role. Its existence does not mean an ordinary separate zażalenie is available against every such order.
- `administrative-decision` fits the later merits decision, not the delay notice or ponaglenie result.
- `dispatch-proof` fits the retained office acknowledgement of the actual ponaglenie. `delivery-proof` fits the identified incoming notice/order/decision service records. Both are evidence sets, not a universal certificate.
- `judicial-complaint` can be linked in the separate court section, with the inactivity subject expressly named. Its main specimen is a merits-refusal complaint and must not be reused as the delay specimen.
- `court-judgment` fits the issued judicial act when explaining the published case. A CBOSA example from another case is not a client-specific judgment.
- `case-file-index` is only a working index. It cannot substitute for actual service records, the authority's file or the submitted ponaglenie. `authority-summons` is not the identity of an Article 36 notice.
