## Proposed article structure

1. **Two kinds of deadline.** The authority's time for deciding and the party's time for acting have different events and consequences. Explain ordinary KPA 35–38, exclusions from the authority's period, and the distinction between bezczynność and przewlekłość. A notice under 36 is not an extension of the party's appeal deadline. Qualify residence processing beside this explanation.
2. **Recipient and valid method of service.** KPA 39/39¹/39⁴ set the channel and the electronic date reference. KPA 40–41 identify the represented party's recipient and changed-address duty. Paper is not universally abolished. Separate receipt by a person authorized to receive from informal forwarding to a client.
3. **Paper service without personal reading.** KPA 42–47: actual acceptance, adult domownik/neighbour/caretaker with the required undertaking and notification, failed attempts and two awizo, and refusal to accept. State the conditions before the fiction. No assumption that a tracking status alone proves all of them. Briefly bound 45/45a corporate recipients, 48 and 49–49b as different mechanisms, not variants of ordinary postal delivery.
4. **Electronic arrival and electronic receipt.** Define ADE and PURDE once. Explain both directions under delivery-act 40–42. An email alert is not the operative receipt; actual receipt means the legally described action making the document available, not an assertion that every word was read. Separate the uncollected-message rule and qualified services. Do not import a paper awizo formula or MOS UPO into PURDE.
5. **Calculate the end, then establish the filing event.** KPA 57 days/weeks/months/years, calendar days inside the period, Saturday/statutory holiday at the endpoint, then the specific preserved-filing methods. A signed draft, dispatch and arrival are different events. Any postal example must name the statutory operator category rather than all couriers.
6. **If the deadline was missed.** First determine whether valid service started it. Restoration under 58 requires a request, plausible lack of fault, seven days from the end of the cause and concurrent performance. The restoration-request deadline cannot itself be restored. Explain 59's ordinary and appeal/complaint competence separately and 60's possible, requested stay of execution. Avoid an invented grant based only on hospitalization.

This is a structure proposal, not a required number of headings. Each section should explain the actual mechanism rather than repeat instructions to check the law.
