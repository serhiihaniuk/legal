# Decisions, procedural orders and other correspondence

Changed on 06.09.2026 following the map contents audit.

## Content and organization

Rewrote `decision-appeal` as a self-contained article about what an authority's document does. It separates the authority's decision or procedural order from the party's appeal or complaint. It defines wezwanie, zawiadomienie and zaświadczenie and explains why their response periods cannot be inferred from the general appeal deadline.

Three explicitly fictional Polish fragments belong to three independent cases served on 03.08.2026. The completed comparison records an address explanation submitted on 12.08, a complaint against denied file access submitted on 07.08, and an ordinary first-instance refusal appeal submitted on 14.08. The respective deadlines are 17.08, 10.08 and 17.08. The fragments are incomplete illustrations, not official forms. Neither the legality of the access restriction nor the merits of the permit refusal are invented as established facts.

The stable node and URL remain unchanged. Related topics lead to the full decision analysis, ordinary remedies, demands, file access and delivery. Detailed appeal drafting remains in chapter nine. The Ukrainian term for postanowienie is aligned with the existing certificate guide: procedural order, `процесуальна ухвала`.

## Official verification

Checked on 06.09.2026 against the [official KPA consolidation](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1691/text/T/D20251691L.pdf), dated 04.12.2025. Read the relevant provisions in articles 36–37, 50, 54, 57, 61a, 74, 77, 101, 104–105, 107, 112, 123–129, 141–144 and 217–219. The [original-act metadata](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/1960/168) was fetched again and retains 2025/1691 as its latest consolidation and 2025/769 as its latest amendment, effective 13.07.2025. No source edition was promoted.

The review preserves these distinctions:

- Article 104 includes decisions ending a case without a merits determination. Discontinuance under article 105 is therefore distinct from refusing the requested permit.
- Article 123 gives the ordinary function of a procedural order with its statutory qualification. Article 61a demonstrates that such an order can refuse initiation instead of merely governing an ongoing case.
- Articles 74 and 101 expressly provide particular complaint rights. The text does not extend article 101 to every resumption-related order. Articles 141–144 distinguish a separate complaint, later challenge with an appeal and the lack of an automatic stay.
- Article 77 allows the authority to change its evidence order. A request explaining the relevance of evidence is not presented as a separately available complaint.
- An article 36 notice is not a permit decision. Article 37 is described only at the general KPA level; the text retains the qualification for special proceedings and dates. It does not settle the outstanding art. 100d review.
- Articles 217–219 distinguish a certificate from its refusal. Article 127 exceptions and article 129 special deadlines limit the ordinary fourteen-day appeal rule. Articles 112 and 126 protect compliance with an incorrect instruction in their stated scope.

The check date applies to this article. The atlas baseline and independent legal acceptance are unchanged.
