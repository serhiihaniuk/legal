- [Regulation 987/2009](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/PL/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02009R0987-20180101), art. 19 ust. 2: competent institution's certificate at the person's or employer's request. Verify the current edition and any later amendments again when authoring.
- [Regulation 883/2004](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/PL/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02004R0883-20190731), arts. 12–13: ordinary posting and work in several countries have different conditions. Ordinary posting requires the relevant employment and employer-activity conditions, an anticipated duration no longer than 24 months and no replacement of another posted worker. The 24-month rule is not the definition of every A1.
- [Regulation 1231/2010](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32010R1231), art. 1 and recitals 10–12: relevant third-country nationals can fall within coordination, subject to lawful Member-State residence and cross-border scope. This grants no entry, residence or labour-market rights. Do not apply it automatically to direct India-to-Poland posting or all non-EU states.
- [Official Polish A1 document published by ZUS](https://www.zus.pl/documents/10182/24154/Formularz_A1_PL.pdf/111d2109-0e74-4c72-9253-dd01cf71859f): three pages. Blocks 1–2 contain holder identity, applicable state and period; blocks 3–5 contain status, employer and work locations; block 6 identifies the issuing institution. The holder's notice distinguishes A1 from EKUZ/S1 healthcare documents.
