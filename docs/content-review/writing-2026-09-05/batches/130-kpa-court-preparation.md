The official [NSA selected-case summary](https://www.nsa.gov.pl/wybrane-orzecznictwo/wybrane-orzecznictwo/skutecznosc-wniesienia-skargi-za-posrednictwem-systemu-e-doreczenia-postanowienia-z-28-pazdziernika-2025-r-i-osk-151625-i-i-osk-151725-oraz-z-20-listopada-2025-r-ii-osk-224225/) discusses I OSK 1516/25, I OSK 1517/25 and II OSK 2242/25. ## Required mechanisms and exact locators

### Act, standing and exhaustion

- PPSA Articles 1–3, pages 1–3: judicial control and specified acts/omissions. Decisions are Article 3 § 2 point 1; eligible orders are separately defined in point 2. Not every interlocutory letter is independently reviewable. Article 5, pages 3–4, also prevents a universal visa/consular model.
- Article 50, page 23: the ordinary complainant's legal interest and separately named institutional/social-organisation standing. Dissatisfaction alone is not universal standing.
- Article 52 §§ 1–2, pages 23–24: exhaustion where a remedy was available. Section 3 allows direct access without reconsideration, subject to its exact consul and foreign-minister/foreigners-law exception. This does not excuse an available ordinary appeal.
- Article 54a, page 26: another party's reconsideration request can cause the complaint itself to be treated as such a request, with notification. If already forwarded, the court returns it under that mechanism. It is not simply waiting for a different request.
- Articles 64a, 64c and 64e, pages 30–31: KPA Article 138 § 2 remand uses sprzeciw, fourteen days and a limited inquiry into remand conditions. Keep this boundary concise.
- Article 53 § 2b, page 24: inactivity complaint after ponaglenie differs from the selected thirty-day route. Current residence-delay exceptions are covered in batch 128; avoid an unqualified duplicate claim.

### Filing, defects and costs

- Articles 34–37, pages 15–17: personal action and eligible court representatives. KPA's broad representation permission does not transfer to court. Eligibility and adequate authority are separate. Article 37 includes its electronic-register proof exception.
- Articles 46–47, pages 18–21: identification, request, signature, attachments and copies; identifiers are conditional, not a universal PESEL requirement. First and later letters differ. Electronic attachments have prescribed signatures and a separate copies rule.
- Article 57, pages 26–27: identify the challenged act, authority and violation of law or legal interest. Explain the connection between a ground, facts and requested result. Do not impose the professional cassation standard on the first complaint.
- Article 53 § 1, page 24: ordinary thirty days from service. Section 4 preserves time when a party files directly with the administrative court before expiry; the court promptly forwards it. This does not cure every unrelated defect or unsupported electronic channel.
- Article 54 §§ 1–3, pages 24–25: through-authority route; ordinary thirty-day transmission with complete ordered files and response, subject to the named sixty-day consular/foreign-minister cases. Autokontrola permits full satisfaction within competence and time; in a decision case the authority annuls its decision and issues a new one. Its response is not the judgment.
- Article 49, page 22, and Article 58, pages 27–28: formal cure and relation-back differ from admissibility. Uncured complaint defects, lateness and other listed obstacles can lead to odrzucenie by order. Not every defect is immediately fatal; not every obstacle is curable.
- Articles 219–220, pages 75–76: court fee and consequences, including rejection for unpaid complaints after the required demand. The foreign-resident/no-domestic-representative payment period is not universally seven days. No amount is proposed without the tariff.
- Articles 243 and 246, pages 81–83: requested legal aid and financial criteria, not automatic free counsel. If describing its cassation-clock effect, retain Article 177 §§ 3–6.

### Inquiry, interim effect and results

- Article 133, pages 53–54: file-based judgment with the stated exceptions. Article 106 § 3, pages 45–46: supplemental documentary evidence needed to resolve important doubts without excessive prolongation. This is not ordinary repetition of the whole administrative evidence process.
- Article 134, page 54: within the case, generally not bound by the grounds/requests/legal basis; retain the specified Article 57a tax-case exception and the limited exception to the prohibition of a worse result. A missed citation does not necessarily prevent ordinary decision review.
- Article 61, pages 28–29: filing does not suspend execution. Authority and court powers differ. After forwarding, the court can suspend on request for danger of significant damage or hard-to-reverse effects, subject to the named exclusions. The request is not the order. Do not imply that every refusal has an executable positive obligation or that the request gives stay/work rights.
- Article 145, page 57: material-law error affecting the result, reopening-type violation, and other procedural error capable of substantial influence. Annulment, invalidity and declaration of unlawful issuance are different results. Section 3 contains its specified administrative discontinuance consequence.
- Article 145a, pages 57–58: a specified manner/result depends on Article 145 § 1 point 1(a) or point 2, the circumstances and absence of administrative discretion. Neither "WSA can never influence the substantive result" nor "successful complaint automatically grants a permit" is accurate.
- Article 151, page 59: oddalenie is failure on merits. Article 58 odrzucenie is a procedural result and a different type of act.
- Articles 152–153, page 60: post-success effect before legal force and binding assessment/instructions, retaining the local-law exclusion, contrary court determination and changed-law qualification as applicable. These differ from Article 61 at filing.

### After the actual court act

- Articles 141–142, page 56: reasons ordinarily ex officio, but after oddalenie a party requests them within seven days of the applicable announcement/service event. The requested reasoned copy is served on that party. Announcement is not the universal cassation start.
- Articles 168–169, pages 63–64: legal force depends on available challenge and specified partial-order rules. WSA can establish it. A printed date or absence of the client's own cassation is insufficient.
- Articles 173–177, pages 64–67: ordinary cassation and exclusions, grounds, professional drafting with named exceptions, thirty days from service of the reasoned act through the issuing WSA. Legal aid has separate timing rules.
- Article 194, pages 70–71: specified WSA orders instead have seven-day zażalenie to NSA. Rejection for lateness, uncured complaint defects and unpaid wpis uses that route; other terminating orders require Article 173. A judicial zażalenie is not a KPA administrative complaint.

## Proposed article and completed example

Six connected sections can explain: the change from administration to legality review; available remedy and exhaustion; filing and admissibility; scope of inquiry and separate suspension; reading the actual result and next remedy; a completed document record. Keep current qualifications visible in the body rather than solely in unrendered claims. Do not turn the orientation into a court-cost tariff or platform manual.

Use a separate ordinary two-instance administrative case. It is not a residence/work-permit claim, tax-interpretation case, remand or inactivity complaint. The person has standing, acts personally, exhausted ordinary appeal, and has no representative, disputed fee exemption or legal-aid application. No exceptional deadline applies. The administrative case began in 2026.

1. The second-instance decision maintaining the first decision is properly served on 3 July 2026. Its instruction identifies the proper WSA, thirty days and the through-authority route. The complaint alleges that an existing document was ignored. The later fictional judgment finds that the administrative reasons did assess it and no basis for granting this complaint is established. No new sector-specific substantive rule is invented.
2. A complete signed paper complaint with required copies is physically accepted by the proper WSA office on 3 August. Its receipt identifies the document. The court fee is duly paid; omit an unverified amount. Although the party used the direct route, Article 53 § 4 preserves time: day thirty was Sunday, 2 August.
3. WSA forwards the complaint on 5 August; the authority receives it on 6 August and sends the complaint, complete file and response to WSA on 20 August. No autokontrola occurs. These are actual acts, not just a reference-number allocation.
4. After a properly conducted hearing with the party present, WSA announces a judgment on 10 September: `oddala skargę`. This is merits dismissal, not rejection for route or lateness. It is an expressly fictional judicial finding, not a model merits determination for an unspecified sector.
5. The party physically files a signed request for reasons on 14 September, before the seven-day endpoint of 17 September. The required fee is duly paid without an invented amount. The court prepares reasons and properly serves the reasoned judgment on 22 September.
6. On 23 September the completed outcome is merits dismissal plus an actually requested and received reasoned judgment. The selected ordinary cassation period ends on 22 October and remains open. No cassation, legal force, NSA result or permit grant is invented. Professional drafting and the actual reasons matter for any further step.
