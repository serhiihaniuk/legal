# Batch 62: residence filing certificates

## Reader question

The permanent-residence and EU-resident cases linked to a guide describing only the temporary-residence certificate. The case references named the correct articles, but the destination did not explain their forms. A reader could not tell whether the document shown belonged to their application.

## Correction

The guide keeps one stable URL and explicitly distinguishes the three statutory variants. Each has its own legal basis and official specimen. It explains issuance through MOS, the difference from UPO, filing versus issue dates, the limits of the certificate, and the separate KPA procedure for ordinary certificates. A completed fictional permanent-residence example identifies the application and both dates. Permanent and resident case rows name their selected variant.
