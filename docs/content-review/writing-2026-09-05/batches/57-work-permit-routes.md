The new work application uses MOS; changing an existing permit uses paper. Exemption under 114(4) does not remove insurance or the full minimum remuneration condition. Decision wording under 118(3–4) is mandatory where its conditions apply. Applicant job-loss notice is 15 working days; the named employer has a separate 15-calendar-day duty. The ordinary 30-day protection starts at job loss and concerns specified revocation grounds. It does not extend validity or authorize work for a new employer.

Blue Card requires a concluded contract of at least six months under the current provision. A discretionary bonus does not establish missing guaranteed remuneration. Decisions issued before 01.06.2025 and proceedings pending at the reform require distinct transitional analysis. Mobility is not unrestricted permission to take any job elsewhere in the EU.

For business, company income, qualifying employment and resources or actions supporting future results are alternatives. Personal income is a separate condition. The regional GUS basis and the company's preceding tax year must not be replaced with the national average or an assumed calendar year. Prokurent appears in both 126 and 142; the role name alone cannot select a route. No actual regional threshold, active quota, tax calculation or specific company's eligibility was determined.
