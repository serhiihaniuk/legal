## Ordinary mechanism preserved in full

Article 35 distinguishes action without unnecessary delay, immediate resolution on the specified available facts/evidence, the ordinary one-month investigation, two-month particularly complicated case, appeal period and simplified proceeding. Special periods remain possible. The body includes the actual Article 35 § 5 exclusions, including public hybrid delivery; it requires an identified period and basis rather than subtracting all time between letters.

Article 36 requires reasons, a new date and instruction about ponaglenie even where the cause is independent of the authority. The notice does not prove that every earlier action was necessary. The module distinguishes the notice from a substantive decision and from an authority summons.

Article 37 § 3a refers to the Article 35 or special-law period. It does not simply refer to every later date named under Article 36. A premature ponaglenie is left unexamined, without the further §§ 4–8 procedure. The module does not offer the label przewlekłość as a way around that rule.

The two seven-day periods under Article 37 §§ 4–5 have different receipt events. Forwarding includes necessary copies and the authority's position. Under § 6, a found violation in an unfinished case requires a completion date. The separate inquiry into causes and culpable persons is distinguished from preventive measures taken when necessary. Section 7 permits a longer completion date only on the stated new-important-fact/evidence condition. Section 8 excludes §§ 4, 6 and 7 where no superior exists; it does not exclude the review period in § 5. It does not require the same superior-authority order issued to oneself.

Article 38 concerns the employee's responsibility. It is not a compensation provision. The separate PPSA Article 149 monetary possibilities are not automatic and are not litigation-cost reimbursement.

## Residence scope and temporal qualifications

The body calls the Article 112a 60/90-day periods general rules, expressly retaining different rules for particular temporary-residence subtypes. It does not claim an exhaustive manual for every subtype. Parent independently identified Articles 137d and 166b as examples of special alternatives; no numerical account of those alternatives is newly authored here.

For post-launch ordinary residence grants, the last specified formal/evidence event controls commencement under Articles 112a(2), 210(2) and 222d(2). The old appearance point has been repealed in the selected new provisions. Article 112a(3) and (3a) have their own host-entity and separated-family events. Appeal formal-defect cure has its own rule. The body identifies these boundaries rather than claiming the filing date universally starts every period. It uses new Article 222d directly, not the repealed Article 223 bridge for a new resident case.

The shared statutory paragraphs identify the Article 100d list, wojewoda stage and current endpoint. The list is not limited by nationality. It does not itself suspend the appeal stage before Szef UdSC, all immigration procedures, separate work-permit proceedings or the party's reply/appeal periods. Acts remain effective and the authority may decide the case. The statutory remedy restrictions are retained beside the later judicial distinction.

## Completed fictional record

The selected case is an ordinary individual KPA proceeding, expressly neither residence nor work permit. It needs investigation but is not particularly complicated; a superior exists; no special period or excluded time occurs.

- 3 June 2026: competent authority receives the formally complete signed request. Necessary evidence is collected by 18 June. The monthly endpoint is 3 July under Article 57 § 3.
- 30 June: proper Article 36 notice is served, naming unfinished evidence assessment, a new 17 July date and the instruction. No decision by that date; no new necessary action explains the continued wait.
- 20 July: the office physically receives a signed, reasoned ponaglenie through the case authority.
- 22 July: forwarding with necessary copies and position. The superior receives it on 23 July.
- 28 July: the superior's order finds non-gross inactivity, sets completion by 11 August for the pending case and orders investigation of causes and culpable persons.
- 7 August: substantive decision issued; proper service on 10 August, before the ordered completion date.

Both seven-day duties are performed within time. The filing comes after both the ordinary and notified dates. The non-gross finding is the expressly fictional order's conclusion, not a rule that every delay of this duration is non-gross. No merits correctness, grant, court complaint or money award is invented. The Polish table is a selected working comparison across six records, not an official form or a purported complete order.
