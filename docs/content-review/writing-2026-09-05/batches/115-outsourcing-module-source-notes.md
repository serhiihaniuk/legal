## Current foreign-employment act

Primary source: [current compiled Dz.U. 2025 poz. 621](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/621/text/U/D20250621Lj.pdf), 100 physical PDF pages, editorial date 17.06.2026. The official PDF and [act API](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/621) were freshly retrieved. The API distinguishes status `obowiązujący` from `inForce: IN_FORCE`. Listed amendments are 1794 effective 27.04.2026, 203 effective 05.03.2026, 473 effective 08.07.2026 and 734 effective 20.06.2026. No additional amending act was listed in this check. A later metadata modification or executive regulation is not treated as another amendment of these provisions.

- Article 2 points 4–8, physical pages 4–5: the contract/legal-relationship requirement, combined foreign-employer criteria, Polish-employer criteria, and imported temporary-employee/user definitions. The article does not classify an employer as foreign merely from its commercial name or a foreign shareholder.
- Article 6(1–2), physical page 15: agency temporary work falls within the relevant statutory permit/declaration models. It is not an additional unrestricted personnel-supply instrument.
- Article 9(1)(1), physical pages 16–17: the application identifies the user and relevant agency information in the temporary-work model. This is distinguished from the subsequently issued permit.
- Article 13(1)(7), physical page 22: the refusal ground concerns the employer that is not a lawful temporary-work agency and the specified work for a third party. It is not rewritten as a ban on every independent service performed at a client's premises.
- Article 17(1)(10), physical page 26: the permit copy is supplied to the user on its request. It is not a universal separate residence attachment.
- Article 32(1–2), physical page 35: ordinary permit conditions and the user identified for temporary work. The module does not invent a mandatory workplace field for every ordinary permit or treat an ordinary employer permit as interchangeable with a user-naming temporary-work permit.
- Article 40(1–3), physical page 38: existing employment and actual foreign work, the distinct posting branches, and the statutory meaning of an export service. The explanation keeps the temporary/occasional service and Polish business-activity qualification. It does not infer posting merely from an invoice between companies.
- Article 44 point 1, physical page 40: the EEA-employer service exemption requires the specified lawful stay, lawful employment within an employment relationship, usual work in that state, and temporary service by the same employer. It is not generalized to every foreign employer or every person working abroad.
- Article 62(1)(4), physical pages 49–50: the declaration's user information in the temporary-work model. Article 65(1)(3), physical page 53, carries the specified refusal grounds by reference. Article 68(1)(8), physical page 55, separately requires the registered declaration copy for the user on request.
- Article 84(11–14), physical page 74: paragraph 12 concerns the referring/entrusting actor under its stated contract and direction conditions, with a minimum 3,000 zł. Paragraph 11 concerns the receiving actor and has its own minimum 6,000 zł. Paragraph 13 applies the per-foreigner minimum expressly to paragraph 1, not paragraph 12. Paragraph 14 has its own list, including paragraph 11. The comparison neither calculates a fine nor finds anyone guilty.

These selected provisions were read from the current official text. The record does not claim a fresh review of every permit condition, exemption, sanction or executive regulation.

## Temporary-work statute

Primary source: [Dz.U. 2025 poz. 236](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/236/text/O/D20250236.pdf), 10 physical pages. The [underlying act API, DU2003/1608](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2003/1608) was checked. It lists 2024/1871 effective 19.03.2025 as the latest amendment and no 2026 amendment. The statute remains in force; the consolidated-text announcement is not confused with the underlying act.

- Articles 1–2, 4–5 and 7, physical page 2: the agency employee, user and statutory kinds of temporary tasks; fixed-term employment and the separate civil-contract referral possibility.
- Articles 8–9 and 9a, physical page 3: prohibited work and written agency/user arrangements. Article 9(2) is information supplied by the user, while paragraphs 1 and 3 concern arrangements. The final module explicitly distinguishes these functions in its first document sentence.
- Articles 11–13, physical page 4: communication to the worker and the employment contract's user, period, work and pay conditions. The agency's commercial agreement and the worker's employment contract have different participants and functions.
- Articles 14 and 14a, physical page 5: user organization, health and safety, working-time duties, the prohibition on onward referral, and the user's personal record of temporary-work periods. The latter is linked to its concrete guide rather than presented as a new universal upload.
- Article 20, physical page 7: the ordinary 18-month/36-month limit, cross-agency and employment/civil combinations, the special continuous replacement model and its break, and the pregnancy-related exception. The ordinary limit is not presented as exhaustive.
- Articles 25 and 25a, physical page 8: the civil-contract model incorporates specified rules appropriately. It is not equated with all employee rights or with a general permission to replace employment by a civil contract.

Actual client direction does not by itself establish that the tasks qualify under article 2(3), that the statutory time limits are met, or that a lawful agency arrangement has been created.

## Agency registration

Primary source: [current compiled Dz.U. 2025 poz. 620](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/620/text/U/D20250620Lj.pdf), 380 physical pages, and its [official API](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/620). Current metadata lists 734 effective 20.06.2026, 451 effective 01.07.2026, 1794 effective 27.04.2026 and 1746 effective 11.12.2025. It also lists future changes 507 effective 14.10.2026 and 1123 effective 01.01.2028. Those future dates are not projected into the September explanation. The original 507 provision changing the market statute was also read; it concerns provisions outside the selected agency rules. This is not a full review of every future reform.

Article 305, physical pages 260–261, distinguishes recruitment from temporary-work services and the ordinary Polish agency's regulated activity. Paragraphs 3–4 require both the specified period of entry and services and an obtained changed entry for the named foreign-worker services. Company age alone is insufficient. The article describes the ordinary Polish agency case rather than generalizing its registration statement to every foreign-provider exception.

Article 445(6), physical pages 376–377, provides the qualified transition for agencies already performing the named services before 01.06.2025. It does not exempt every pre-existing company from the special scope requirement. The selected fiction does not rely on that transition. Current KRAZ scope and Polish company identity retain distinct roles.

## Specific evidence and posting distinction

[Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1629](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1629/text/O/D20251629.pdf) was freshly read. Paragraph 2 point 2, physical page 1, requires the user's confirmation of the agreed referral for the selected agency permit procedure. Paragraph 7 point 2, physical page 3, supplies the corresponding declaration requirement. Paragraph 8 governs documentary handling; paragraph 9 sets commencement on 01.12.2025. The [API](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1629) lists the regulation as in force with no amendment listed.

The [Dolnośląski UW permit guidance](https://www.gov.pl/web/dolnoslaski-uw/zezwolenie-na-prace-cudzoziemca-na-rzecz-polskiego-podmiotu-powierzajacego-prace-cudzoziemcowi), retrieved text line 193, expressly gives an agency/user agreement as a possible confirmation. This supports the narrow statement that one suitably specific physical document may perform both functions. The entire webpage was not accepted as a current universal attachment checklist. No mandatory national confirmation form or authenticated submission was inspected or claimed.

[Current posting statute, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1682](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/1682/text/O/D20251682.pdf), article 3 point 4 on physical page 3, includes the specified agency posting model. The [underlying act API, DU2016/868](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2016/868) lists 621 effective 01.06.2025 as its latest amendment, with no 2026 amendment listed. Posting and agency direction therefore address different legal questions and may overlap. The module does not add an A1, PIP-notice or residence-procedure manual.

## Official interpretation and its limits

The [MRPiPS letter of 03.04.2026 published by the Senate](https://www.senat.gov.pl/gfx/senat/userfiles/_public/k11/komisje/kp/75/2/odp._mrpips_03-04-2026.pdf) was freshly read, especially physical pages 2–3 and 6–7. The opening proposed definitions belong to the petition described by the ministry. They are not current statutory definitions. The ministry's own discussion supports examining the service obligation, responsibility, actual direction and evidence rather than a contract's label alone. Its discussion of possible contract types does not make every outsourcing agreement an `umowa o dzieło`.

The ministry's statements are attributed official guidance. Court decisions quoted within its letter were not independently reviewed and are not presented as independently verified holdings. The article does not infer legal classification from hourly billing, a shared workplace or one supervisor instruction alone.

## Completed comparison

The separate fictional case concerns Polish client U and Polish contractors A and C, neither acting as an agency. Their 30 July contracts both describe independent packaging services. The compared work occurs from 3 to 7 August 2026. Each team includes one adult Indian employee with its contractor's employment contract and ordinary permit covering the selected employer, packaging work and August period. Compatible lawful national-visa stay is stipulated separately. No independent work-access exemption is assumed.

A's supervisor assigns its people's hours and tasks. U specifies access, delivery and quality. A assigns and bears the cost of the 6 August correction following U's 5 August complaint. In C's records, U's manager assigns individual shifts, stations, tasks and the 6 August rework; C's coordinator forwards those instructions. On 10 August, the completed comparison supports A's independent service organization and identifies the contradiction between C's contract and actual direction. C and U cancel further work in that unchanged organization before the next shift. This action does not terminate the worker's employment contract or establish a new agency arrangement.

The Polish sample is a preparer's selected comparison table, not an official form, employer certificate or inspection decision. Its five rows contain 15 cells. It establishes a bounded organizational conclusion. It does not certify A's full compliance, classify C as a lawful agency, establish that all tasks meet the temporary-work definition, revoke a permit or impose a sanction. No real identifiers are used.
