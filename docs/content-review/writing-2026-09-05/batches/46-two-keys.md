# Batch 46: lawful stay and permission to work

Rewrote /map/two-keys as a standalone explanation of two legal questions, rather than a demand for two documents. The article distinguishes a status that gives free labour-market access, work under a permit or registered declaration, the compatibility of the residence basis and the conditions of the actual job. It explains the limited conclusion available from a residence application while the proceeding is pending.

Three completed examples cover Polish permanent residence, an ordinary employer change and a work permit whose stated period has ended. The employer-change example includes a filled four-row working note. Missing evidence is identified without turning it into a definitive finding of unlawful stay or work. The former generic UKR statement is removed; the current temporary-protection topic remains related reading, rather than this foundation article duplicating its conditions.
