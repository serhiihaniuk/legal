## Proposed explanation

Use approximately five substantive sections, adjusted to the actual prose rather than a fixed template:

1. "KPA регулює кілька видів проваджень". Closely read the opening list. Explain the individual, legally specified matter and the public-authority role, including legally entrusted other bodies. Briefly identify competence disputes, administrative monetary penalties and European cooperation without expanding those regimes.
2. "Рішення вирішує справу, а мовчання потребує спеціальної підстави". Define the output and its scope. Keep the distinction between ordinary non-response and a legally enabled silent procedure next to the explanation. Do not import a one-month automatic residence grant.
3. "Довідка підтверджує, а не надає дозвіл". Explain the purpose of a requested certificate and the need to establish its statutory basis or legal interest. A document bearing `zaświadczenie` may have a separate special-law procedure. If the seven-day certificate rule is mentioned, attribute it to the ordinary KPA procedure rather than every document with that name.
4. "Skarga і wniosek мають різні предмети". Distinguish a complaint about administration from an organizational proposal, and both from an application seeking an individual entitlement. Explain the procedural-stage qualification for a complaint about an individual case. Do not conflate skarga under Dział VIII, zażalenie, odwołanie and skarga do WSA.
5. "Межі кодексу читають разом із визначеннями". Give the relevant article 3 qualifications, the real role of article 4 and a useful example of article 5 terminology. Retain one short paragraph about a special rule displacing a particular question, rather than repeating the existing material-versus-procedure lesson.

Keep detailed timing, competent complaint recipients, formal filing requirements and remedies outside this introductory scope unless the final example needs them. Each additional operational claim needs its own exact source. In particular, the article 222 content rule does not make every form, signature or filing channel irrelevant.
