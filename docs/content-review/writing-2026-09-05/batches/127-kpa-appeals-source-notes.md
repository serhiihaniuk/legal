## Amendment 769 and visible transitions

Metadata: <https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/769>. It reports promulgation on 12 June 2025 and entry into force on 13 July 2025.

Text: <https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/769/text/O/D20250769.pdf>.

- Page 3, art. 1 pkt 5–9: added 109 §3–4, replaced 124, amended 125, replaced 132 §1–2 and added 139a. The module explains the current appeal mechanisms; it does not attribute every adjacent provision to this amendment.
- Page 9, art. 15 pkt 1–4: PPSA judicial-sprzeciw changes, the reconsideration exception and its limits, and added 64f for remand postanowienia.
- Page 19, art. 31 ust. 1: old KPA wording for cases initiated and unfinished before commencement. The module's hypothetical case begins in 2026.
- Page 19, art. 31 ust. 2 pkt 7: separate preservation of previous PPSA wording for pending court proceedings, with the specified art. 39 exception.
- Page 20, art. 39: that exception concerns PPSA 239 §1 pkt 2, a costs rule. It is not a blanket exception making the new remedies applicable to every older court proceeding.
- Page 21, art. 48: general 30-day commencement and the listed exceptions. The selected art. 1 and art. 15 changes commenced on 13 July 2025.

The court transition is rendered beside the judicial boundary. It does not imply that the date of opening an administrative case alone determines the edition governing a later court proceeding.

## Judicial boundary and later amendments

Metadata: <https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2002/1270>. Reopened during preparation and authorship. It reports `IN_FORCE`, consolidation status and `changeDate: 2026-07-20T09:47:38`. Listed later amendments include DU/2026/846 and DU/2025/1882. Their relevant effects were checked rather than treating the printed consolidated-text date as the latest legal state.

Text: <https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/143/text/T/D20260143L.pdf>, printed 10 February 2026.

- Pages 14–15, 52–54a: exhaustion of administrative remedies, the specific optional reconsideration route and consular/foreign-minister exceptions, ordinary 30-day court complaint and filing through the issuer. The module does not teach a complete court filing workflow or the full multi-party interaction in 54a.
- Pages 17–18, 64a–64f: sprzeciw from a 138 §2 remand, 14 days, route through the issuer, the court's limited inquiry into remand prerequisites, and the corresponding remedy for remand postanowienia. The body does not rename sprzeciw as ordinary skarga.
- The official text contains a future alternative electronic court-filing rule for 1 October 2029. It was not imported as current administrative or court filing guidance.

## Completed example and each document's role

The hypothetical expressly stipulates that actual use of identified premises during July 2026 is a relevant substantive fact in its ordinary administrative proceeding. It does not invent a universal housing requirement or name an unverified specific permit. There is one party, no representative, no waiver, no special remedy period and no immediate execution.

The lease and the party's explanation already exist. The witness examination and inspection were requested but not performed. The decision's central conclusion about non-use is unsupported. The example does not claim those future evidence actions have already established use or that a later inspection necessarily proves July conditions.

| Event            | Confirmed fictional date and consequence                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First refusal    | Issued 27 August; served Monday 31 August. Issue and service are separate.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Party filing     | Signed appeal actually received by the first authority on Friday 4 September, evidenced by its receipt. Under 57/129 the 14-day period runs from 1 through Monday 14 September.                                                                             |
| Transmission     | The first authority did not issue a new decision in autokontrola and transferred the appeal with the records on Thursday 10 September. This falls within its separate seven-day period from receipt on 4 September.                                         |
| Appellate result | Remand decision issued Thursday 17 September and served Monday 21 September. It revokes in full and remands under 138 §2. It identifies procedural breaches, material unresolved fact-finding and why the scope is not merely supplementation under 136 §1. |

The sole party did not request the special 136 §2 investigation route. Absence of that request alone does not justify remand, which the analysis states expressly. No new first-instance outcome, positive permit or court judgment is supplied. Sprzeciw has not been filed. The module does not fabricate a future filing on the basis of the open judicial period.

The 12 Polish specimen cells are a completed working comparison, not an authority template. The remand cell contains a typed provision reference. Dates, party assumptions and bounded outcome agree with the surrounding Ukrainian explanation.

Document IDs used in their actual roles:

- `administrative-decision`: an issued decision, including the first refusal and the distinct appellate type explained in the article.
- `administrative-appeal`: the party's appeal, not an application for a permit, a draft or proof of receipt.
- `delivery-proof`: service of the identified act; not the printed issue date.
- `dispatch-proof`: proof of actual filing; not the signature date or the later result.
- `procedural-order` and `procedural-complaint`: conditional explanation of postanowienie and zażalenie. Neither is inserted into the selected appeal packet.
- `judicial-complaint`: the distinct ordinary court remedy only. The example contains no such filed document, and the guide is not used as a false destination for sprzeciw.

The hypothetical lease is named plainly because its premises are not specified as residential. No residential-lease guide is mislabelled as a universal commercial or unspecified lease guide. There are no new catalog IDs or registry edits.
