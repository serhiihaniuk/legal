# Batch 49: entry, current stay and return after travel

Rewrote /map/entry-current-basis as a standalone article. It separates admission at the border, the legal basis for remaining in Poland on the date of analysis and the documents needed for a later return. The date and territorial scope of each basis explain why a previous lawful entry cannot answer all three questions.

The page explains visa validity versus permitted days, the rolling short-stay calculation, and the separate role of a national visa. It connects the conditions of a timely temporary-residence application to lawful stay, including the exception for suspension at the party's request. It distinguishes the current certificate from UPO and from a residence card.

A completed five-row timeline follows entry on a D visa, timely filing without formal defects, a later certificate, visa expiry and a proposed journey. It reaches separate conclusions for lawful stay on 10 September and the unconfirmed basis for returning. It does not assume that every nationality requires a visa or rule out statutory entry exceptions.

The route and chapter remain unchanged. Existing visa, residence-card, certificate and stay-history guides are linked. Detailed visa, visa-free, pending-stay and special-status rules remain in their own topics. No renderer changes were needed.
