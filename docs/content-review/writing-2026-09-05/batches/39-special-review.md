## What changed

Rewrote three complete map articles against legal-explanatory-writing. Their existing IDs and URLs remain stable.

- `extraordinary-wsa` compares ordinary appeals, special administrative review and court review. It removes the assumption that every court case follows a final administrative decision. It explains exhaustion, the reconsideration exception and its consular limitation, inactivity complaints, sprzeciw against a remand decision, parallel proceedings and execution. A filled fictional comparison separates a disputed assessment from a previously unknown old fact and records a timely court filing without promising a permit.
- `reopening` explains the grounds, the receiving and deciding authorities, the one-month filing period and the separate limits on repeal. Its filled example distinguishes an old insurance payment, a later certificate and a new employment contract. The completed procedural result is opening the review, not cancellation of the refusal.
- `invalidity` explains the seven statutory grounds, ten-year and thirty-year limits, irreversible legal effects and authority. A filled comparison establishes why a second decision concerns the same already resolved case. The fictional outcome invalidates that second decision without treating the first decision as removed.

Both detailed pages correct the old discretionary description of suspension. KPA requires the competent authority to suspend execution when the respective statutory probability condition is met. Submission itself is not suspension. The overview distinguishes that rule from the court's powers under PPSA.

Removed the generic court-database search link as evidence for an unspecified judgment. Gross violation and irreversible legal effects remain expressly identified as assessments requiring concrete legal analysis. No independent legal acceptance is claimed.
