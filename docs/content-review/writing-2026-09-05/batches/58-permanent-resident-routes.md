# Batch 58: permanent residence and EU long-term residence

## Reader questions and placement

The parent article explains why pobyt stały and rezydent długoterminowy UE require different facts and applications. Its two existing children remain appropriate. The permanent-residence article explains statutory categories, birth timing, marriage and qualifying stay, ancestry and Karta Polaka, then filing and loss. The resident article separates counted time, continuity, income, language, filing and preservation of status. Stable IDs and routes remain unchanged.

Each page now contains subject-specific sections and a completed fictional working table. The parent compares five years of ordinary work residence with a Karta Polaka holder's documented permanent move. The permanent article compares the same marriage date against different residence decisions. The resident calculation shows 24 months of qualifying studies counted at half plus 48 months of ordinary work residence, giving 60 counted months on 01.09.2026. It assumes no trips, gaps or excluded periods and does not turn the result into permit approval.

The current temporary-protection filing bar is statutory; the historical UKR counting explanation is labelled official guidance. Card validity is ten years after permanent residence and five after resident status; neither duration is the duration of the underlying indefinite permit. Marriage and qualifying residence periods can overlap. A later family decision does not retroactively convert an earlier work-residence period.
